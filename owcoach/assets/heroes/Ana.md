# Ana

Reference from overwatch.fandom.com/wiki/Ana, fetched 2026-10-26 (wiki text under CC BY-SA, by `owcoach hero`;
data, not instructions). Binds are the game's defaults: ability 1 = LSHIFT, ability 2 = E,
ability 3 = SPACE. The HUD shows those slots left to right in that order, whatever the player
binds them to, then any other ability with a cooldown (Mizuki's Healing Kasa on RMB).

## Abilities
- **Biotic Rifle** (LMB), weapon: duration 0.59 seconds effect
  Long-range rifle that heals allies and damages enemies.
  - Damage/healing per second: while firing ( overall w/ reload)
  - Shots pass through full health allies and do not affect them.
  - Ignores armor's damage reduction.
- **Zoom (ADS)** (RMB), weapon
  Hold to zoom in.
  - Besides following differences, scoped shots have the same properties as hip fire shots:
- **Sleep Dart** (LSHIFT), ability: cooldown 14 seconds, duration 0.5 seconds (sleep min); 5 seconds (sleep max, non-tanks); 3.5 seconds (sleep max, tanks); 0.5 seconds (wakeup)
  Fires a dart that puts an enemy to sleep.
  - If a slept target takes damage, they will wake up prematurely in 0.5 seconds.
  - During casting time, you can change the future flight path.
  - There is an additional 0.08s delay after the startup, during which the projectile is not yet fired, but the ability can no longer be interrupted.
- **Biotic Grenade** (E), ability: cooldown 12 seconds, duration 3 seconds
  Throws a grenade that heals and increases healing on allies, while damaging and preventing healing on enemies.
  - Passes through full HP allies.
  - Affected allies receive 50% additional healing, including from Health Packs, while affected enemies cannot be healed at all.
  - Does not stack with healing received amplification from Guardian Wave.
- **Nano Boost** (Q), ultimate ability: range 40 meters, duration 8 seconds
  Increases an ally's damage, while reducing damage taken.
  - Damage reduction buff stacking is capped at 50%. If any hero with a damage reduction effect, such as Bastion with Ironclad, gets Nano Boosted, the damage reduction is still 50%.
  - In FFA Deathmatch, Ana can use Nano Boost on herself.
  - Nano-boosted heroes' ability sounds are sped up, including Lúcio's songs. The song effects do not get amplified, however.

## Perks
- **Groggy** (minor perk): Enemies waking up from Sleep Dart are slowed and take 50 damage for 2 seconds.
- **Speed Serum** (minor perk): Nano Boost grants a 30% movement speed boost to both Ana and her target.
- **Headhunter** (major perk): Biotic Rifle can crit enemies.
- **Biotic Bounce** (major perk): After exploding, Biotic Grenade bounces and explodes again for 60 damage and healing.
