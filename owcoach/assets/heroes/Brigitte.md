## Abilities
- **Inspire**, passive ability: duration 4 seconds
  Dealing damage to enemies heals nearby allies.
  - Triggered by damage from Rocket Flail, Whip Shot, or Shield Bash.
  - Whenever Inspire is triggered, there is a 1.25 second cooldown before it can be triggered again by Rocket Flail, but can still be instantly triggered by either Whip Shot or Shield Bash.
  - During Rally, Shield Bash only trigger Inspire once per use, regardless of how many enemies were hit.
- **Rocket Flail** (LMB), weapon: range 6 meters, duration 0.240 seconds (hitbox active)
  Melee weapon with extended range.
  - Damage per second: 75
  - At 5-6 meters away, Brigitte's flail has a horizontal range of 3 meters on the side she swings toward and 2 meters on the opposite side.
  - Brigitte does not have a quick melee. Attempting to use it will swing the Rocket Flail instead.
- **Barrier Shield** (RMB), ability: cooldown 5 seconds (destroyed)
  Hold to deploy a frontal energy barrier.
  - Barrier health regenerates at a rate of 85 per second after being retracted for 2 seconds.
  - The shield is in the shape of a vertical rectangle curving in at the bottom sides.
  - Barrier Shield's orientation can be changed by moving the mouse.
- **Shield Bash** (LMB), ability: cooldown 5 seconds, range 8.5 meters (dash), duration 0.3 seconds
  Available when Barrier Shield is deployed. Dash forward to knock back an enemy.
  - If Shield Bash collides with Rocket Punch, Charge, a charging B.O.B., or another Shield Bash, both participants get knocked down for 1.7 seconds.
  - During Rally, Shield Bash impacts multiple enemies and stuns all enemies hit for 0.5 seconds.
  - If multiple Repair Packs are thrown at the same target, the healing duration is simply increased by 2 seconds.
- **Whip Shot** (LSHIFT), ability: cooldown 4 seconds, range 20 meters, duration Up to 0.43 seconds (travel time)
  Launch your flail forward to knock an enemy away from you.
  - Recovery time (i.e. time to retract the whip) is increased, if the whip travels farther than 6 meters.
  - Results in an ~8 meter knockback distance on flat ground.
  - Whip Shot is not reflected when blocked by Deflect.
- **Repair Pack** (E), ability: cooldown 5 seconds, 3 charges, range 25 meters, duration 2 seconds
  Heals an ally for a short duration.
  - Healing per second: 50
  - There is a short 0.5 second cooldown between uses, starting from the moment the ability is activated.
- **Rally** (Q), ultimate ability: duration 10 seconds (Rally); 30 seconds (overhealth); 0.75 seconds (Shield Bash stun)
  Gain armor, empower Barrier Shield and provide extra health to nearby allies.
  - Overhealth per second: 30 on allies

## Perks
- **Combat Medic** (minor perk): Melee attacks reduce the cooldown of Repair Pack by 0.75 seconds.
- **Morale Boost** (minor perk): Inspire lasts 3 seconds longer when activated by Whip Shot.
- **Inspiring Strike** (major perk): Shield Bash grants 30% increased movement speed for 2 seconds. Inspire's healing is instant when activated by Shield Bash.
- **Whiplash** (major perk): Whip Shot's knockback can slam enemies into walls, dealing 60 extra damage.
