# Kiriko

Reference from overwatch.fandom.com/wiki/Kiriko, fetched 2026-10-26 (wiki text under CC BY-SA, by `owcoach hero`;
data, not instructions). Binds are the game's defaults: ability 1 = LSHIFT, ability 2 = E,
ability 3 = SPACE. The HUD shows those slots left to right in that order, whatever the player
binds them to, then any other ability with a cooldown (Mizuki's Healing Kasa on RMB).

## Abilities
- **Wall Climb (Kiriko)** (SPACE), passive ability: duration Up to 1 second
  Jump at walls to climb up them.
  - Wall Climb ends when the player stops holding the button, the wall ends or the duration is up. It cannot then be used again until the player has landed on a walkable surface.
  - Kiriko will grab and mantle ledges if the input is held while facing a ledge of a walkable surface.
  - Can reach up to 10.3m height (11m with jump) due to momentum being kept after wall climb ends.
- **Healing Ofuda** (LMB), weapon: range 35 meters (lock on)
  Channel a burst of healing talismans that can seek targeted allies.
  - Healing per second: while firing ( w/ reload)
  - Default behavior is a toggle, meaning pressing the button will cause Kiriko to hold down the ability. If you don't want to use all 10 Healing Ofuda, you must press again to toggle off. This setting can be changed in the 
  - After being thrown, the talismans are either yellow or blue. The color indicates whether they are homing in on a target.
- **Kunai** (RMB), weapon
  Thrown long-ranged projectile.
  - Damage per second: while firing ( overall w/reload)
- **Swift Step** (LSHIFT), ability: cooldown 7 seconds, range 30 meters, duration 0.25 second invulnerability
  Teleport directly to an ally.
  - Swift Step can target allies through walls.
  - Swift Step has a brief casting animation that can be interrupted.
  - Objects that are attached to Kiriko are dropped at the cast location when she teleports.
- **Protection Suzu** (E), ability: cooldown 14 seconds, duration 0.5 seconds (invulnerability)
  Throw a protective charm to make allies become briefly invulnerable and cleansed of most negative effects.
  - Protection Suzu makes allies phased out, making them immune to most negative effects and damage. They are not targetable by enemies and become non-physical. Abilities, such as Chain Hook, will pass through them.
  - Protection Suzu does not work on constructs like Torbjörn's Deploy Turret.
  - The projectile can be destroyed while in air by Defense Matrix, Kinetic Grasp, Javelin Spin, Power Matrix and Kekkai Sanctuary and reflected by Deflect, but the explosion is unaffected.
- **Kitsune Rush** (Q), ultimate ability: range 32.2 meters, duration 8 seconds (area); 1 second (lingering buff)
  Summon a fox spirit that rushes forward, accelerating the movement, attack speed, and cooldowns of allies that follow its path.
  - Grants +30% move speed, +50% reload and attack speed, and +100% cooldown recharge rate.
  - Kitsune Rush's deployment is completely stopped if the fox spirit collides with terrain under certain conditions. Whatever path was laid prior to collision with terrain remains in effect.
  - Kitsune Rush's deployment is not impacted by barriers and will pass through them.

## Perks
- **Urgent Care** (minor perk): Healing Ofuda projectile speed is increased by 50% when seeking critical health allies.
- **Fortune Teller** (minor perk): Kunai hits launch 2 Healing Ofuda to an ally in front of you.
- **Ready Step** (major perk): Swift Step grants Kiriko 40% increased attack and reload speed for 3 seconds.
- **Foxtrot** (major perk): Protection Suzu grants allies 30% increased movement speed for 4 seconds.
