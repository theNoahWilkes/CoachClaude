## Abilities
- **Berserker**, passive ability: duration 2 seconds
  Gain temporary health when dealing critical damage.
  - After 2 seconds, the overhealth starts decaying at a rate of 5 hp/s. Dealing more critical damage will reset this condition.
  - Both headshots as well as hitting a burning target with "Cha-Cha" counts toward this.
  - The center of Overrun's stomp counts as critical damage and gives overhealth accordingly to damage dealt.
- **Incendiary Chaingun** (LMB), weapon: duration 4 seconds (damage over time)
  Automatic weapon that ignites enemies on repeated impacts.
  - A.K.A "Gunny"
  - Damage per second:
  - Gunny and Cha-Cha can be fired one at a time or simultaneously:
- **Volatile Chaingun** (RMB), weapon
  Automatic weapon that deals critical damage to burning enemies.
  - A.K.A "Cha-Cha"
  - Fortify does not prevent critical hits to an ignited Orisa.
  - Hitting enemies that are burning from other abilities (such as Ashe's , Torbjörn's or Anran's ) will also cause Cha-Cha to deal critical damage.
- **Overrun** (LSHIFT), ability: cooldown 6 seconds, range 4.4 meters (charge, min); 28 meters (charge, max); 6.5 meters (stomp), duration 0.32 seconds (charge, min); 2 seconds (charge, max); 0.6 seconds (stomp) ; 1 second (knockdown)
  Charge forward to stomp and launch enemies. You are unstoppable while charging.
  - Cooldown starts when the ability ends.
  - Mauga cannot shoot or use other abilities (except Cardiac Overdrive) during Overrun.
  - Mauga can turn while charging using the directional keys.
- **Cardiac Overdrive** (E), ability: cooldown 12 seconds, duration 3 seconds
  Gain damage reduction and nearby allies heal themselves by dealing damage.
  - Cooldown starts when the ability ends.
  - Despite the description stating it only affects allies, Mauga also gains the effect of Cardiac Overdrive while it is active.
  - Allies that enter the radius after the ability has been activated will also gain its effects.
- **Cage Fight** (Q), ultimate ability: duration 8 seconds
  Deploy a barrier that traps yourself and enemies. Automatically reloads Mauga's Chainguns at the start of the ultimate.
  - Can be interrupted by crowd control abilities during deployment, causing the Ultimate to be wasted.
  - Can be canceled early by pressing the ability key again.
  - Activating Cage Fight restores the ammo of Incendiary Chaingun to full.

## Perks
- **Kinetic Bandolier** (minor perk): Overrun reloads up to 150 ammo during the first second of charging.
- **Pyromaniac** (minor perk): Igniting enemies with Incendiary Chaingun grants 50 overhealth.
- **Firewalker** (major perk): Overrun ignites enemies hit.
- **Combat Fuel** (major perk): Critical hits grant Mauga 3 temporary overhealth on Cardiac Overdrive's next use, up to 150 overhealth.
