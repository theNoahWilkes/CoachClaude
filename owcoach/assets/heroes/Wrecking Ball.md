# Wrecking Ball

Reference from overwatch.fandom.com/wiki/Wrecking_Ball, fetched 2026-10-26 (wiki text under CC BY-SA, by `owcoach hero`;
data, not instructions). Binds are the game's defaults: ability 1 = LSHIFT, ability 2 = E,
ability 3 = SPACE. The HUD shows those slots left to right in that order, whatever the player
binds them to, then any other ability with a cooldown (Mizuki's Healing Kasa on RMB).

## Abilities
- **Quad Cannons** (LMB), weapon
  Automatic assault weapons.
  - Damage per second: while firing ( overall w/reload)
  - There is a 0.16 seconds delay after releasing the fire button before it can be used again.
  - First two shots have perfect accuracy; bullet spread reaches max at 30 shots.
- **Grappling Claw** (RMB), ability: cooldown 5 seconds (after fireball); 1 second (no fireball), range 22.2 meters (max); 6 meters (retracted), duration 8 seconds (max grapple time after fireball)
  Launch a grappling claw to rapidly swing around the area. Enables high speed collisions to damage and knockback enemies.
  - Wrecking Ball automatically enters Roll when Grappling Hook is fired and will remain in it while and after grappling.
  - Wrecking Ball can manually retract the line by holding the Jump button.
  - If the line is more than 6 meters long and Wrecking Ball moves closer to the claw, the line retracts and stays retracted.
- **Roll** (LSHIFT), ability: cooldown 0.4 seconds
  Transform into a ball and increase maximum movement speed.
  - Can be cast while in midair.
  - Roll form base speed is the same in all directions (i.e. he is not slower going backwards).
  - Roll form rolls faster when going downhill. Going uphill does not affect the speed.
- **Adaptive Shield** (E), ability: cooldown 15 seconds, duration 7 seconds (self); 4 seconds (allies)
  Create temporary extra health that increase with more nearby enemies. Some of the health can be transferred to allies.
  - Cannot be activated while using melee, casting Minefield or Piledriver.
  - Adaptive Shields can be reactivated once to redistribute portion of Wrecking Ball's overhealth evenly to all other allied heroes within the radius.
  - B.O.B. counts as an enemy for creating extra health as well as an ally that health can be transferred to. Constructs, such as Deploy Turret, do not.
- **Piledriver** (CTRL), ability: cooldown 8 seconds, duration 0.8 seconds (windup delay); 0.75 seconds (enemy air lock)
  Slam into the ground below to damage and launch enemies upward.
  - This ability is bound to Crouch/Toggle Crouch, and replaces Wrecking Ball's ability to crouch on the ground.
  - Wrecking Ball must be airborne and at a minimum altitude of 3.5 meters of the surface directly below the center of his model to activate.
  - Wrecking Ball will be transformed to Roll form immediately upon activation.
- **Minefield** (Q), ultimate ability: duration 16 seconds (on ground); 1.25 seconds (arming time)
  Deploy a massive field of proximity mines.
  - Deploys 15 proximity mines around him, arranged into three rings of 1/6/8 mines.
  - Activating Minefield in the air increases the spread.
  - Mines can pass through barriers when deployed, but explode upon contact once armed.

## Perks
- **Steamroller** (minor perk): Roll impacts deal 100% more damage to Tanks.
- **Multi-Ball** (minor perk): Press Ultimate within 5 seconds after using Minefield to deploy 7 additional mines.
- **Hang Time** (major perk): Piledriver winds up longer, gaining air control and dealing up to 50% more damage.
- **Adaptive Barrier** (major perk): Adaptive Shield generates a 1 second barrier on activation.
