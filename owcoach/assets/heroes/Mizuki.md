# Mizuki

Reference from overwatch.fandom.com/wiki/Mizuki, fetched 2026-10-26 (wiki text under CC BY-SA, by `owcoach hero`;
data, not instructions). Binds are the game's defaults: ability 1 = LSHIFT, ability 2 = E,
ability 3 = SPACE. The HUD shows those slots left to right in that order, whatever the player
binds them to, then any other ability with a cooldown (Mizuki's Healing Kasa on RMB).

## Abilities
- **Remedy Aura**, passive ability: range 12 meters, duration 1.75 seconds (aura decay delay)
  Heal nearby allies. Healing scales with resource generated by dealing damage and healing.
  - The aura boundary is visible only to Mizuki. Allies cannot see it.
  - When at high aura, a deep rhythmic beat can be heard when healing.
  - It takes 9 non-critical hits from Spirit Glaive to reach 100% aura (with 8 reaching 96% aura).
- **Spirit Glaive** (LMB), weapon: range 30 meters, duration 0.5 seconds (damage over time)
  Throw a spinning blade that impacts enemies and rapidly dealing damage.
  - Damage over time is unaffected by Amplification Matrix and does not deal critical damage, but can be affected by other damage amplifications.
- **Healing Kasa** (RMB), ability: cooldown 6 seconds, 2 charges, range 30 meters (initial throw); 12 meters (bounce range from target), duration 0.25 seconds (heal over time duration)
  Throw your hat to heal an ally, bouncing to nearby allies and healing you when it returns.
  - Bounces up to 3 times, automatically targeting the lowest health ally within range after the first target.
  - Cannot bounce back to an ally it has already hit.
  - The hat cannot be targeted through terrain, but once the hat has locked on to an ally, it can follow them through walls.
- **Katashiro Return** (LSHIFT), ability: cooldown 12 seconds, duration 5.5 seconds (paper doll); 0.25 seconds (invulnerability); 0.17 seconds (leap)
  Leap forward, leaving behind a paper doll. Reactivate to return and gain increased movement speed while active.
  - The cooldown begins when Katashiro Return is deployed.
  - Causes a short leap in the direction the player is travelling upon activation, followed by a lingering movement speed buff while active.
  - The paper doll left behind is considered a strong projectile and cannot be destroyed, absorbed or reflected.
- **Binding Chain** (E), ability: cooldown 12 seconds, range 20 meters, duration 1.6 seconds (hinder, non-tanks); 1.2 seconds (hinder, tanks)
  Launch a tethering chain that hinders the first enemy hit.
  - The ability tethers up to 2.5 meters.
  - Cooldown is triggered even if no target is hit.
  - Any mid-air heroes hit will be plunged to the ground to the tether.
- **Kekkai Sanctuary** (Q), ultimate ability: duration 6 seconds; 0.3 seconds (linger)
  Create a sanctuary that heals allies within and absorbs enemy projectiles that pass through it.
  - The damage absorption works similarly to Defense Matrix, meaning that melee and beam weapons, area effects and strong projectiles pierce through it.
  - The startup is instant, but the area is created after a 0.048 second delay.

## Perks
- **Wellspring** (minor perk): Remedy Aura resource generation increased by 25%.
- **Exposed Soul** (minor perk): Hitting an enemy with Binding Chain increases your damage dealt to them by 30% for 2 seconds.
- **Resonant Return** (major perk): Healing Kasa bounces one additional time. Each bounce increases its healing by 10.
- **Quickstep** (major perk): While Katashiro Return is active, allies within Remedy Aura gain 20% increased movement speed.
