# Reaper

Reference from overwatch.fandom.com/wiki/Reaper, fetched 2026-10-26 (wiki text under CC BY-SA, by `owcoach hero`;
data, not instructions). Binds are the game's defaults: ability 1 = LSHIFT, ability 2 = E,
ability 3 = SPACE. The HUD shows those slots left to right in that order, whatever the player
binds them to, then any other ability with a cooldown (Mizuki's Healing Kasa on RMB).

## Abilities
- **The Reaping**, passive ability
  Dealing damage heals you.
  - Counts all damage from Reaper, even from quick melee or environmental kills.
  - Does not generate ultimate charge.
- **Hellfire Shotguns** (LMB), weapon
  Short-range spread weapons.
- **Dire Triggers** (RMB), ability: cooldown 6 seconds
  Use (RMB) to fire a volley with long range accuracy from both Hellfire shotguns.
  - Damage per pellet is equal to 60% of the base Hellfire Shotguns' damage.
  - Both shotguns will still fire even if there is only one shot left in the magazine.
- **Wraith Form** (LSHIFT), ability: cooldown 8 seconds, range 23.1 meters, duration 0.4 seconds (min); 3 seconds (max)
  Move faster and become invulnerable, but you cannot shoot.
  - Wraith Form is a channeled ability; Reaper cannot fire or use other abilities while it's active.
  - Reaper becomes phased out, cleansing him of status effects and becoming immune to enemy damage and abilities.
  - Automatically reloads Hellfire Shotguns to full ammo.
- **Shadow Step** (E), ability: cooldown 10 seconds, range 35 meters, duration 0.53 seconds (teleport); 0.98 seconds (invulnerability)
  Teleport to a targeted location.
  - Cooldown starts when the ability finishes casting.
  - Can only target walkable surfaces.
  - Can be used while Reaper is in the air, which will reduce Reaper's gravity by 70% while its being cast.
- **Death Blossom** (Q), ultimate ability: duration 3 seconds
  Damage all nearby enemies.
  - Death Blossom locks on to all players and objects (barriers, turrets, traps, etc.) in its range that are within line of sight, and periodically fires toward each target at the same time.
  - Cannot be manually canceled.
  - Death Blossom is a channeled ability; Reaper cannot fire or use other abilities while it's active and it is interrupted if Reaper is stunned, knocked down, or hacked, including subsidiary effects.

## Perks
- **Lingering Wraith** (minor perk): Leaving Wraith Form grants 30% movement speed for 2 seconds.
- **Soul Reaving** (minor perk): Collect Soul Globes from dead enemies to restore 50 health.
- **Shadow Blink** (major perk): Shadow Step's cast time and cooldown are 25% faster, but the range is reduced by 25%. Cooldown 10 → 7.5 seconds.
- **Trigger Finger** (major perk): Refresh Dire Triggers' cooldown whenever using an ability or reloading.
