# Zenyatta

Reference from overwatch.fandom.com/wiki/Zenyatta, fetched 2026-10-26 (wiki text under CC BY-SA, by `owcoach hero`;
data, not instructions). Binds are the game's defaults: ability 1 = LSHIFT, ability 2 = E,
ability 3 = SPACE. The HUD shows those slots left to right in that order, whatever the player
binds them to, then any other ability with a cooldown (Mizuki's Healing Kasa on RMB).

## Abilities
- **Snap Kick** (V), passive ability
  Quick melee damage increased by 50%, and its knockback is significantly increased.
  - Results in up to ~7 meters knockback on flat ground.
  - Vertical component of the knockback is reduced the further the target is.
- **Orb of Destruction** (LMB), weapon
  Energy projectile weapon.
  - Damage per second: while firing ( overall w/reload)
  - Damage per second: (w/ discord) ( overall w/reload)
- **Orb of Destruction Alt Fire** (RMB), weapon
  Charge to release more projectiles.
  - Damage per second: while firing ( overall w/reload)
  - Damage per second: while firing (w/ discord) ( overall w/reload)
- **Orb of Harmony** (LSHIFT), ability: range 40 meters, duration 3 seconds (if out of sight)
  Launch this orb at an ally to heal them.
  - Orb of Harmony remains on the target indefinitely, while they stay within line of sight of Zenyatta.
  - Multiple orbs from different Zenyattas stack additively.
- **Orb of Discord** (E), ability: range 40 meters, duration 1.5 seconds (if out of sight)
  Launch this orb at an enemy to increase the damage they take.
  - Orb of Discord remains on the target indefinitely, while they stay within line of sight of Zenyatta.
  - When Orb of Discord is removed from a target, they cannot be targeted by it again for 6 seconds.
- **Transcendence** (Q), ultimate ability: duration 6 seconds
  Become invulnerable, move faster, and heal nearby allies.
  - Zenyatta becomes invulnerable, preventing damage and making him immune to slows, stuns and knockdowns.
  - Zenyatta cannot shoot or use abilities during Transcendence.
  - Orb of Destruction's ammunition is restored upon activation.

## Perks
- **Discordant Repair** (minor perk): Zenyatta gains 15% lifesteal against enemies affected by Discord Orb.
- **Ascendance** (minor perk): Activate and hold Double Jump to hover for up to 3 seconds.
- **Focused Destruction** (major perk): Secondary fire charges 20% faster and can store 1 extra Orb of Destruction.
- **Dual Harmony** (major perk): Gain a 2nd Harmony Orb but they both heal for 70% effectiveness.
