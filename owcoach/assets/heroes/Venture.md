# Venture

Reference from overwatch.fandom.com/wiki/Venture, fetched 2026-10-26 (wiki text under CC BY-SA, by `owcoach hero`;
data, not instructions). Binds are the game's defaults: ability 1 = LSHIFT, ability 2 = E,
ability 3 = SPACE. The HUD shows those slots left to right in that order, whatever the player
binds them to, then any other ability with a cooldown (Mizuki's Healing Kasa on RMB).

## Abilities
- **Clobber** (V), passive ability: cooldown 1.2 seconds, range 3 meters, duration 0.7 seconds
  Quick melee deals more damage.
  - The initial hit actually does less damage than standard melee (from 40 to 30). An additional 40 damage is dealt over time (for 70 damage total).
  - From activation, there is a ~0.2 second active window to deal the initial damage, after which smaller periodic damage is dealt over the remaining duration of the attack.
  - Damage is not reflected when blocked by Deflect.
- **Explorer's Resolve**, passive ability: duration 3 seconds
  Using abilities grants temporary shields.
  - Total amount of shield health gained is capped at 75.
  - Shields are granted when the ability successfully starts. E.g. in case of Burrow, upon entering underground.
  - Clobber does not trigger Explorer's Resolve. (Covered in Dirt Major perk only)
- **Smart Excavator** (LMB), weapon: range 16 meters
  Launch a seismic charge that bursts after a short distance.
  - Projectile explodes upon hitting an enemy, terrain or upon reaching its maximum range.
  - Enemies hit by the shot are knocked back.
  - Explosions do not deal self-damage, but will cause a knockback.
- **Drill Dash** (RMB), ability: cooldown 8 seconds, range 10 meters (regular); 15 meters (burrowed), duration 0.45 seconds (unburrowed); 0.9 seconds (burrowed)
  Dash forward while above or underground, pushing enemies back. The cooldown is accelerated while underground.
  - Cooldown starts when the ability is activated.
  - While Burrow is active, the cooldown of Drill Dash is accelerated by 90%.
  - Upon hitting an enemy for the first time, they take a small amount of damage. If the enemy stays in contact, they will take additional damage over the remaining duration.
- **Burrow** (LSHIFT), ability: cooldown 8 seconds, duration 4 seconds (burrowed); 0.2 seconds (emerging, min charge); 0.65 seconds (emerging, max charge); 1.3 seconds (emerging, max delay))
  Move underground and become invulnerable. Emerge to deal damage.
  - Reveals enemy health bars without having dealt damage to them.
  - Cooldown starts when the ability finishes casting.
  - Venture receives a movement speed penalty while casting, replaced by a movement speed buff once underground.
- **Tectonic Shock** (Q), ultimate ability: duration 7 seconds
  Send out damaging shockwaves.
  - Tectonic Shock cannot be activated or fired while burrowed.
  - Quick melee/Clobber cannot be used during Tectonic Shock.
  - Tectonic Shock fires shockwaves that travel along ground.

## Perks
- **Deep Burrow** (minor perk): Drill Dash distance is 75% longer while while burrowing.
- **Excavation Exhilaration** (minor perk): While Tectonic Shock is active, cooldowns refresh 300% faster.
- **Smart Extender** (major perk): Empower SMART Excavator with E to increase its maximum projectile range by 100% for 4 seconds. Cooldown 12 seconds.
- **Covered in Dirt** (major perk): Dealing damage with Clobber grants up to 40 Explorer's Resolve shields.
