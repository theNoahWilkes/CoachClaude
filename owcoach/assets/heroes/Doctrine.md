# Doctrine

Reference from overwatch.fandom.com/wiki/Doctrine, fetched 2026-10-26 (wiki text under CC BY-SA, by `owcoach hero`;
data, not instructions). Binds are the game's defaults: ability 1 = LSHIFT, ability 2 = E,
ability 3 = SPACE. The HUD shows those slots left to right in that order, whatever the player
binds them to, then any other ability with a cooldown (Mizuki's Healing Kasa on RMB).

## Abilities
- **Eternal Scepter** (LMB), weapon
  Mid-range weapon that heals allies and damages enemies.
  - Damage/healing per second: 82.5 while firing (73.3 w/ reload)
  - Infused shot does not crit
- **Infuse** (RMB), weapon: cooldown 8 sec, 2 charges
  Add a powerful effect to your next ability.
- **Shrouded Momentum**, ability: cooldown 8 sec, duration 1.8sec
  Rush horizontally with damage reduction.; Infused: Gain free-flight.
  - Flying backwards ignore the speed penalty of moving backwards
- **Invigorating Drones**, ability: cooldown 14 sec, range 30m (cast), duration 5 sec
  Deploy drones to an ally, granting attack speed and healing.; Infused: Also deploy drones to yourself.
  - Follows allies outside of range and line of sight if applied initially.
  - Multplies beam damdges by an additional 30%
- **Deliverance** (Q), ultimate ability: range 12m (cast), duration 6 sec (swarm); 6 sec (overhealth & debuff); 5 sec (overhealth & debuff decay)
  Launch a swarm of drones that reduces enemy maximum health and grants allies overhealth.
  - Enemy health reduction :
  - Deliverance swarm is a strong projectile. Individual drones are normal projectiles
  - EMP destroys the Deliverance

## Perks
- **Saving Grace** (minor perk): Invigorating Drones heal for 40 health when applied.
- **Sanguine Siphon** (minor perk): Heal for 50% of damage dealt with Infused Eternal Scepter.
- **Transfusion** (major perk): Reduce Infuse's cooldown from damage and healing dealt.
- **Cost of Life** (major perk): Sacrifice 25 maximum health. Eternal Scepter healing is increased by 20%.
