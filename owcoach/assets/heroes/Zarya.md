# Zarya

Reference from overwatch.fandom.com/wiki/Zarya, fetched 2026-10-26 (wiki text under CC BY-SA, by `owcoach hero`;
data, not instructions). Binds are the game's defaults: ability 1 = LSHIFT, ability 2 = E,
ability 3 = SPACE. The HUD shows those slots left to right in that order, whatever the player
binds them to, then any other ability with a cooldown (Mizuki's Healing Kasa on RMB).

## Abilities
- **Energy**, passive ability
  Damage blocked by barriers increases Particle Cannon damage.
  - Energy is gained by blocking damage with Particle Barrier and Projected Barrier (max. 100%).
  - Energy starts decaying 2 seconds after last gaining Energy at a rate of 2% per second.
  - Energy instantly resets to 0 upon death. (Resurrect does not restore Energy.)
- **Particle Cannon** (LMB), weapon: range 15 meters
  Short-range linear beam weapon.
  - Damage per second: 95 while firing (0% charge) (73.07 overall w/reload)
  - Damage per second: 175 while firing (100% charge) (134.61 overall w/reload)
- **Particle Cannon Alt Fire** (RMB), weapon
  Energy grenade launcher.
  - Damage per second: 55 while firing (0% charge) ( overall w/reload)
  - Damage per second: 110 while firing (100% charge ( overall w/reload)
  - Grenades can still be fired even with less than 25 ammo, dealing full damage.
- **Particle Barrier** (LSHIFT), ability: cooldown 12 seconds, 2 charges, duration 2.25 seconds
  Create a damage barrier around you.
  - Charges are shared with Projected Barrier. The charge cooldown of the next charge is set depending on which ability was used.
  - Zarya gains 0.2% Energy per damage blocked by the barrier, or 1% Energy per 5 damage blocked.
  - Another Particle Barrier cannot be cast while Zarya is affected by her own Personal Barrier, Projected Barrier from another Zarya or enemy Domina's Panopticon.
- **Projected Barrier** (E), ability: cooldown 9 seconds, 2 charges, range 30 meters, duration 2.25 seconds
  Create a damage barrier around an ally.
  - Charges are shared with Particle Barrier. The charge cooldown of the next charge is set depending on which ability was used.
  - Projected Barrier behaves identically to Particle Barrier, excluding following differences:
- **Graviton Surge** (Q), ultimate ability: duration 4 seconds
  Launch a gravity well that pulls enemies to it.
  - All movement abilities are disabled within the effect of Graviton Surge.
  - The projectile is blocked by barriers, activating upon contact, but the area of effect pierces barriers.
  - The projectile can be destroyed by Defense Matrix, Kinetic Grasp, Javelin Spin, Power Matrix and Kekkai Sanctuary and reflected by Deflect before it impacts, but the area is unaffected.

## Perks
- **Spotter** (minor perk): Projected Barrier activates ally health regeneration and increases their movement speed by 20%.
- **Jump-Ups** (minor perk): Secondary Fire's self-knockback is increased by 75%.
- **Energy Lance** (major perk): Particle Cannon's beam pierces enemies.
- **Extra Oomph** (major perk): While a Barrier is active, dealing damage with Particle Cannon’s beam generates energy.
