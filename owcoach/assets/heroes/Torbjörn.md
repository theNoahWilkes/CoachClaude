# Torbjörn

Reference from overwatch.fandom.com/wiki/Torbjörn, fetched 2026-10-26 (wiki text under CC BY-SA, by `owcoach hero`;
data, not instructions). Binds are the game's defaults: ability 1 = LSHIFT, ability 2 = E,
ability 3 = SPACE. The HUD shows those slots left to right in that order, whatever the player
binds them to, then any other ability with a cooldown (Mizuki's Healing Kasa on RMB).

## Abilities
- **Rivet Gun** (LMB), weapon
  Slow firing, long-ranged weapon.
  - Damage per second: while firing ( overall w/reload)
  - Automatically reloaded if Torbjörn has his Forge Hammer equipped for 2 seconds.
  - Turret will target enemy hit by primary fire if within range and line of sight.
- **Rivet Gun Alt Fire** (RMB), weapon
  Inaccurate but powerful short-range weapon.
  - Damage per second: while firing ( overall w/reload)
  - Automatically reloaded if Torbjörn has his Forge Hammer equipped for 2 seconds.
- **Forge Hammer** (LMB), weapon: range 2.5 meters, duration 0.192 seconds (hitbox active)
  Swing to repair your turret or damage an enemy.
  - Damage per second:
  - Weapon swap time is 0.4 seconds.
  - Torbjörn cannot use quick melee with Forge Hammer equipped. Attempting to will swing the hammer normally instead.
- **Deploy Turret** (LSHIFT), ability: cooldown 5 seconds (default); 12 seconds (in combat), range 15 meters (deploying turret); 40 meters (attack range), duration Until destroyed or replaced
  Deploy a self-building turret.
  - Damage per second:
  - Turret deploy can be thrown to a maximum distance of 15 meters on ground level.
  - Turret automatically attacks the closest enemy within line of slight and continues attacking that enemy until it is either dead or out of sight.
- **Overload** (E), ability: cooldown 10 seconds, duration 6 seconds
  Gain additional extra health as well as improved attack, movement, and reload speed.
  - Damage per second: while firing primary
  - Damage per second: while firing secondary
  - Damage per second: ~135 while using forge hammer
- **Molten Core** (Q), ultimate ability: duration 5.5 seconds (max transformation); 10 seconds (pools)
  Create pools of molten slag that damage enemies. Deals additional damage to armor.
  - Torbjörn's weapon is temporarily replaced by his claw, which shoots out balls of molten slag.
  - The projectiles bounce from walls, and upon landing on ground create pools that deal damage over time.
  - Against armor, the pools deal damage per second.

## Perks
- **Hammer Time** (minor perk): Move 20% faster while Forge Hammer is equipped.
- **Pre-Heated** (minor perk): Molten Core activates Overload.
- **Anchor Bolts** (major perk): Deploy Turret's throw range is increased by 25% and it can now attach to walls and ceilings.
- **Overloaded Turret** (major perk): Overload upgrades your Turret for 5 seconds, increasing its health and damage.
