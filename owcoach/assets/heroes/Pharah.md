# Pharah

Reference from overwatch.fandom.com/wiki/Pharah, fetched 2026-10-26 (wiki text under CC BY-SA, by `owcoach hero`;
data, not instructions). Binds are the game's defaults: ability 1 = LSHIFT, ability 2 = E,
ability 3 = SPACE. The HUD shows those slots left to right in that order, whatever the player
binds them to, then any other ability with a cooldown (Mizuki's Healing Kasa on RMB).

## Abilities
- **Hover Jets** (SPACE), passive ability: duration Up to 2 seconds (continuous flight); 0.3 seconds (energy regen delay)
  Hold to hover.
  - Activated by holding jump button while in the air.
  - Allows Pharah to gain height when held.
  - Refuels 45% per second automatically while on ground, excluding the regen delay, takes 2.22 seconds to refill from empty to full.
- **Rocket Launcher** (LMB), weapon
  Long-range explosive projectile weapon.
  - Damage per second: 150 while firing (114 overall w/reload)
  - Fire recovery stops while using melee
  - Explosive Damage and knockback decreases with distance from the explosion, reaching 0% at the edge of the blast radius.
- **Jet Dash** (RMB), ability: cooldown 9 seconds, duration 0.85 seconds
  Boost horizontally.
  - Provides a quick horizontal boost in the direction Pharah is moving.
- **Jump Jet** (LSHIFT), ability: cooldown 14 seconds, range 8.4 meters (vertical), duration 0.85 seconds (launch); 6 seconds (overfill)
  Fly rapidly upwards. Grants some fuel.
  - Restores 50% of Hover Jets fuel over 0.9 seconds and can temporarily overfill the maximum fuel amount.
  - Getting stunned does not interrupt the ability after it has been activated.
  - Jump Jet movement is influenced by diretional inputs.
- **Concussive Blast** (E), ability: cooldown 8 seconds
  Launch an explosive blast to knock back enemies.
  - Also knocks back Pharah herself, without dealing damage.
  - Mostly deals horizontal knockback.
  - The projectile can be destroyed while in air by Defense Matrix, Kinetic Grasp, Javelin Spin, Power Matrix and Kekkai Sanctuary and reflected by Deflect, but the explosion is unaffected.
- **Barrage** (Q), ultimate ability: duration 2.5 seconds
  Hover in place and launch a continuous volley of mini-rockets.
  - Damage per second: 1050
  - Barrage is a channeled ability; while it is active Pharah cannot use other abilities and becoming stunned, knocked down or hacked immediately ends the ability, consuming the ultimate charge.
  - Pharah remains stationary while the ability is active, but can turn.

## Perks
- **Concussive Force** (minor perk): Concussive Blast deals up to 30 explosion damage.
- **Helix Shields** (minor perk): Convert 125 health to shields. Direct hits with the Rocket Launcher triggers passive shield regeneration.
- **Fuel Stores** (major perk): Jet Dash grants 50% fuel. Maximum overfuel is increased by 100%.
- **Rocket Salvo** (major perk): After using a movement ability, the next primary fire shoots two spiraling mini-rockets, each dealing 30 damage.
