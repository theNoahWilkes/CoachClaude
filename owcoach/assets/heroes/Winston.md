# Winston

Reference from overwatch.fandom.com/wiki/Winston, fetched 2026-10-26 (wiki text under CC BY-SA, by `owcoach hero`;
data, not instructions). Binds are the game's defaults: ability 1 = LSHIFT, ability 2 = E,
ability 3 = SPACE. The HUD shows those slots left to right in that order, whatever the player
binds them to, then any other ability with a cooldown (Mizuki's Healing Kasa on RMB).

## Abilities
- **Tesla Cannon** (LMB), weapon: range 8 meters
  Electric frontal-cone weapon.
  - Damage per second: 70 while firing ( overall w/reload)
  - Barrier Projector and Jump Pack can be used without interrupting fire.
  - Ignores armor damage reduction.
- **Tesla Cannon Alt Fire** (RMB), weapon: range 40 meters
  Hold to charge, release to fire focused electricity
  - Damage per second:
  - The amount of ammo drained increases as the ability is charged, up to 12.
  - The cannon automatically fires 1.5 seconds after reaching maximum charge.
- **Jump Pack** (LSHIFT), ability: cooldown 5 seconds
  Leap forward into the air. Landing on an enemy damages them.
  - Cooldown starts immediately after activation.
  - The damage scales based on distance to target upon landing.
  - Enemies that collide with Winston mid-leap take 1 damage.
- **Barrier Projector** (E), ability: cooldown 10 seconds, duration 7 seconds
  Deploy a protective energy dome.
  - Cooldown starts immediately after activation.
  - Barrier becomes active after a 0.13 second delay.
- **Primal Rage** (Q), ultimate ability: range 4 meters (melee), duration 10 seconds (transformation)
  Gain immense health, but you can only leap and punch enemies.
  - Winston's maximum health is increased by 700 and his health and armor are fully restored upon activation, resulting in 1175 total hit points (1325 in role queue): 975 health (1125 in role queue) and 200 armor.
  - Jump Pack's cooldown is reset upon activation and when Primal Rage ends and lowered to 2 seconds while Primal Rage is active.
  - Barrier Projector and Tesla Cannon cannot be used during Primal Rage.

## Perks
- **Electric Charge** (minor perk): Winston gains 10% movement speed for each enemy damaged by his primary fire, up to 30%.
- **Heavy Landing** (minor perk): During Primal Rage, Jump Pack's damage and area increase by up to 75% while airborne.
- **Chain Lightning** (major perk): Fully charged Secondary Fire hits bounce to up to 2 additional targets.
- **Revitalizing Barrier** (major perk): Barrier Projector heals allies within it for 35 health per second.
