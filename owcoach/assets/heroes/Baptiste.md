# Baptiste

Reference from overwatch.fandom.com/wiki/Baptiste, fetched 2026-10-26 (wiki text under CC BY-SA, by `owcoach hero`;
data, not instructions). Binds are the game's defaults: ability 1 = LSHIFT, ability 2 = E,
ability 3 = SPACE. The HUD shows those slots left to right in that order, whatever the player
binds them to, then any other ability with a cooldown (Mizuki's Healing Kasa on RMB).

## Abilities
- **Exo Boots** (CTRL), passive ability: range 9.1 meters (full charge)
  Hold crouch to jump higher.
  - Takes 0.7 seconds for the boots to fully charge.
  - Baptiste must be standing on walkable surface to charge Exo-Boots.
  - Charging stops if crouch is released or Baptiste becomes airborne, and the boots will remain charged for 1 second. The charge will reset if Baptiste starts charging the boots again during this time.
- **Biotic Launcher** (LMB), weapon
  Three-round burst gun.
  - Damage per second: ~127.5 while firing (109 overall w/reload)
  - Baptiste's crosshair moves up as he fires, then comes back down after firing.
  - Reloading Biotic Launcher replenishes the ammo for both the primary and alternate fire.
- **Biotic Launcher Alt Fire** (RMB), weapon
  Lob a healing projectile that heals all allies near the impact.
  - Healing per second: 66.66 - 99.99 (indirect - direct) while firing (57.09-85.64 overall w/reload)
  - Cannot heal self.
  - Projectile passes through full-health allies.
- **Regenerative Burst** (LSHIFT), ability: cooldown 12 seconds, duration 4 seconds
  Activate to heal yourself and nearby allies instantly, with additional healing over time. Instant heal is doubled for targets less than half health.
  - Healing per second = 12.5
  - The heal-over-time effect is applied once upon activation. Allies can get out of range and continue to get healed.
  - Although the ability has a casting animation, the healing over time is applied instantly, but begins healing after a delay, which means it cannot be interrupted by stuns, hacks or death.
- **Immortality Field** (E), ability: cooldown 20 seconds, duration 5 seconds (generator); 5.5 seconds (field)
  Toss a device that prevents allies from dying. The device can be destroyed.
  - Prevents the HP of Baptiste and his allies from going below 25%. It even prevents environmental death, but healing the HP lost this way will not give ultimate charge.
  - Heroes below the minimum HP threshold will instantly be healed to the threshold when entering the field. Biotic Grenade can amplify or block this; if the healing is blocked, Immortality Field will still prevent all incom
  - The device can be thrown and bounced off walls. The arc is identical to that of Biotic Launcher's alternate fire, except Immortality Field collides with the skybox.
- **Amplification Matrix** (Q), ultimate ability: range 35 meters, duration 10 seconds
  Project a matrix that doubles the damage and healing of allied projectiles.
  - Damage per second: ~258.2 while firing (Biotic Launcher)
  - Healing per second: 133.2 - 199.8 (indirect - direct) while firing (Biotic Launcher)
  - Before being deployed, the orientation can be changed by pressing the ultimate key again.

## Perks
- **Assault Burst** (minor perk): Regenerative Burst now provides Baptiste with 20% increased attack speed for 3 seconds.
- **Expanded Field** (minor perk): Immortality Field radius increased by 30%.
- **Automated Healing** (major perk): Using any ability triggers Baptiste's Shoulder Turret, periodically firing up to 3 shots at allies, each restoring 40 health.
- **Rocket Boots** (major perk): While airborne from Exo Boots, use (SPACE) to dash horizontally. Cooldown 6 seconds.
