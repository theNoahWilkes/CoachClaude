# Junker Queen

Reference from overwatch.fandom.com/wiki/Junker_Queen, fetched 2026-10-26 (wiki text under CC BY-SA, by `owcoach hero`;
data, not instructions). Binds are the game's defaults: ability 1 = LSHIFT, ability 2 = E,
ability 3 = SPACE. The HUD shows those slots left to right in that order, whatever the player
binds them to, then any other ability with a cooldown (Mizuki's Healing Kasa on RMB).

## Abilities
- **Adrenaline Rush**, passive ability
  Heal from all damage over time dealt by wounds.
  - Self-healing is not reduced by the global healing reduction passive.
  - Adrenaline Rush instantly heals for the remaining wound damage when an enemy dies with wounds on them.
  - Healing received from Adrenaline Rush grants ultimate charge.
- **Scattergun** (LMB), weapon
  Pump action shotgun.
  - Damage per second: while firing ( w/ reload)
  - Fire recovery stops while using melee.
- **Jagged Blade** (RMB), ability: cooldown 6 seconds, range 60 meters, duration 3 seconds
  Active: Throw the blade. Re-activate to pull it back with any impaled enemy.; Passive: Wound enemies hit by Quick Melee or Throw, dealing damage over time.
  - The cooldown starts when the blade is thrown.
  - The blade can be recalled anytime after the throw, even while still in mid-air.
  - The blade is automatically recalled, if has travelled for 1.5 seconds (60m) without hitting an enemy or terrain.
- **Commanding Shout** (LSHIFT), ability: cooldown 10 seconds, duration 4 seconds
  Grants temporary health and movement speed to yourself and allies.
  - Cooldown starts when the duration ends.
  - Commanding Shout can be activated while casting any other abilities without interrupting them.
  - Movement speed bonus counts towards global movement speed buff cap.
- **Carnage** (E), ability: cooldown 9 seconds; -2 seconds (per enemy hit), range 4.5 meters, duration 3 seconds (wound); 0.144 seconds (hitbox active)
  Wound all enemies in front of you, dealing damage over time and reducing the cooldown for each enemy hit.
  - Carnage is not reflected when blocked by .
  - No wound is inflicted, if the attack is blocked by Deflect, Javelin Spin or Warding Stance.
- **Rampage** (Q), ultimate ability: range 25 meters, duration 4.5 seconds (DoT); 0.7 seconds (dash)
  Charge foward. Wounds enemies, dealing damage over time and preventing them from being healed.
  - Walls and enemy barriers can line of sight block the effects from being applied in area.
  - Life Grip cannot be used on Junker Queen during Rampage. Activating Rampage also interrupts the pull.
  - Rampage's movement speed buff does not count towards global movement speed buff cap.

## Perks
- **Rampant Charge** (minor perk): Gain Unstoppable and refreshes all cooldowns by 6 seconds when using Rampage.
- **Battle Shout** (minor perk): Commanding Shout fully reloads Scatter Gun and increases allied reload speed by 50%.
- **Willy-Willy** (major perk): When recalling Jagged Blade its radius is increased by 100% and it deals 30 additional impact damage.
- **Savage Satiation** (major perk): Carnage's impact damage gains 100% lifesteal.
