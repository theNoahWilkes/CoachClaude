# Moira

Reference from overwatch.fandom.com/wiki/Moira, fetched 2026-10-26 (wiki text under CC BY-SA, by `owcoach hero`;
data, not instructions). Binds are the game's defaults: ability 1 = LSHIFT, ability 2 = E,
ability 3 = SPACE. The HUD shows those slots left to right in that order, whatever the player
binds them to, then any other ability with a cooldown (Mizuki's Healing Kasa on RMB).

## Abilities
- **Biotic Grasp** (LMB), weapon: range 15 meters, duration 3 seconds (HoT); 0.45 seconds (energy regen delay)
  Hold to heal all allies in front of you. Consumes biotic energy.
  - Causes a heal over time that begins upon the last impact of the spray healing. This allows Moira to heal without expending resources.
  - Passes through allies and enemies, but not enemy barriers.
  - Line of sight blocked by terrain and terrain-like objects.
- **Biotic Grasp Alt Fire** (RMB), weapon: range 20 meters
  Long range beam weapon. Damage dealt heals you and replenishes your biotic energy.
  - Biotic Grasp has a 0.7 meter target acquisition radius against enemy heroes.
  - Self-healing and damage are completely separate. Altering the damage does not affect the self heal.
  - Generates 21.875 biotic energy per second. When combined with the passive regeneration, this becomes 25.715 biotic energy per second (or 16% per second).
- **Fade** (LSHIFT), ability: cooldown 6 seconds, range 15 meters (max), duration 0.752 seconds + 0.048 s. recovery
  Disappear, move faster, and become invulnerable, but you cannot shoot.
  - Moira becomes invisible and phased out, cleansing her of most negative status effects and making her immune to damage.
  - Moira can still pick up Health Packs during Fade.
  - While in Fade, Moira jumps higher than normal, reaching up to 4 meter height.
- **Biotic Orb** (E), ability: cooldown 8 seconds, range 4 meters (damage); 5 meters (healing), duration 7 seconds (max)
  Launch a bouncing sphere that either heals nearby allies or damages nearby enemies.
  - Bounces off walls and pierces heroes.
  - The orb itself pierces barriers, but does not target enemies or allies through them.
  - Dealing damage with Biotic Orb restores a small amount of Biotic Energy.
- **Coalescence** (Q), ultimate ability: range 30 meters, duration 8 seconds
  Fire a beam that heals allies and damages enemies.
  - Passive Biotic Energy regen rate is increased by +200% during the ultimate.
  - Coalescence's healing has a miss forgiveness behavior: If no allied heroes are hit, the beam's healing will be increased by 50% for a short duration depending on how long it hasn't hit an ally for.
  - Moira can use Fade while channeling Coalescence. The beam will be temporarily disabled while in Fade.

## Perks
- **Destruction's Divide** (minor perk): Coalescence can be toggled between pure healing or pure damage, with 30% greater effect.
- **Ethical Nourishment** (minor perk): Biotic Orb’s first 30 healing is instant on each ally it encounters.
- **Reversal** (major perk): Reactivating Biotic Orb reverses its direction.
- **Phantom Step** (major perk): Fade lasts 0.5 seconds longer and boosts jump height by 50%.
