## Abilities
- **The Viper** (LMB), weapon
  Semi-automatic rifle.
  - Ashe reloads The Viper one bullet at a time in rapid succession, instead of all at once.
  - Unlike most weapons, The Viper is semi-automatic instead of fully automatic.
  - Spread resets after 1.5 seconds since the last unscoped shot. Spread starts on the 3rd shot and reaches its maximum upon the 6th shot.
- **Take Aim (ADS)** (RMB), weapon
  Hold to zoom in, increasing damage and accuracy but slowing rate of fire.
  - Ashe can jump while aiming down sights without interrupting it.

- **Coach Gun** (LSHIFT), ability: cooldown 10 seconds, range 15 meters (enemy knockback); 9.4 meters (self-knockback)
  Blast enemies in front of you and knock yourself backwards.
  - During cast time, you can change the shot direction.
  - The bullets do not need to hit anything in order to apply the knockback to Ashe.
  - Can be used while rooted or hindered, however it will only knock back the enemy and not Ashe.
- **Dynamite** (E), ability: cooldown 12 seconds, duration 2 seconds (explosion delay on ground); 5 seconds (burn)
  Throw an explosive that detonates after a short delay or immediately when shot.
  - Can be shot at with The Viper or Coach Gun to detonate immediately.
  - The burn also affects barriers and turrets.
  - The burn is a damage over time effect. It is removed immediately by phased out status, such as Protection Suzu, Particle Barrier or upon taking a Health Pack.
- **B.O.B.** (Q), ultimate ability: range 70 meters (charge); 40 meters (arm cannons), duration 1.5 seconds (immobilize base); 0.5 seconds (immobilize flying); 4 seconds (charging); 10 seconds (after stopping)
  Deploy Bob. He charges forward and knocks enemies into the air, then attacks with his arm cannons.
  - Damage per second: 136 while active
  - B.O.B. appears slightly to the right of Ashe and lands 5 meters in front of her, then charges in the direction Ashe is looking at. Once B.O.B. bumps into an enemy or a wall, or travels 65 meters, B.O.B. halts and begins 
  - Unlike other turrets, B.O.B. has spread, meaning if you are far away enough, not every single bullet will land, this is most noticeable at his max range.

## Perks
- **Remote Detonator** (minor perk): After using Dynamite, pressing Ability 2 again causes it to explode after a 0.5 second delay.
- **Double-Barreled** (minor perk): Knocking an enemy back with Coach Gun briefly allows you to use it one extra time within 2 seconds.
- **Viper's Sting** (major perk): Hitting 2 consecutive scoped shots on a target deals 25 extra damage.
- **Airburst** (major perk): Dynamite has a 40% increased detonation radius while airborne and refunds 6 ammo when thrown.
