# Orisa

Reference from overwatch.fandom.com/wiki/Orisa, fetched 2026-10-26 (wiki text under CC BY-SA, by `owcoach hero`;
data, not instructions). Binds are the game's defaults: ability 1 = LSHIFT, ability 2 = E,
ability 3 = SPACE. The HUD shows those slots left to right in that order, whatever the player
binds them to, then any other ability with a cooldown (Mizuki's Healing Kasa on RMB).

## Abilities
- **Augmented Fusion Driver** (LMB), weapon
  Automatic heat-based weapon.
  - Max spread is reached after 21 shots.
  - Uses a heat-based mechanic instead of ammo/reload.
- **Energy Javelin** (RMB), ability: cooldown 7 seconds, duration 0.45 seconds (impact stun); 0.4 seconds (wall stun)
  Launch your javelin at an enemy to stun and knock them back. More effective if the enemy hits a wall.
  - Has a slider angle of 45°, meaning if the target enemy hits a wall at an angle greater than 45° from the surface normal, they will not receive the effects of a wall impact.
  - If the javelin hits D.Va's Self-Destruct while it is moving, the Mech will be stunned and will stop moving.
- **Fortify** (LSHIFT), ability: cooldown 15.5 seconds, duration 4 seconds
  Gain temporary health, reducing all damage taken and becoming unstoppable.
  - Orisa is immune to critical hits while active.
  - "Unstoppable" causes Orisa to become immune to crowd control status effects, including slow, root, knockback, stun, knocked down, gravity and Hack ability lockout.
  - Orisa can be lifted by Lifeweaver's Petal Platform and pulled by Life Grip while Fortified.
- **Javelin Spin** (E), ability: cooldown 8 seconds, range 3.5 meters; 180° (matrix angle), duration 0.5 seconds (min); 1.75 seconds (max); 2 seconds (movement speed)
  Spin your javelin to destroy projectiles and block melee attacks, while also pushing enemies and increasing forward speed.
  - Deals up to 100 damage over the duration.
  - Orisa cannot shoot or use quick melee during Javelin Spin.
  - Using Energy Javelin interrupts Javelin Spin.
- **Terra Surge** (Q), ultimate ability: duration 0.75 - 4 seconds (charge time)
  Sweep in enemies and anchor down, gaining the effects of Fortify and charging up a surge of damage. Use Primary Fire to unleash the surge early.
  - When activated, Orisa starts generating charge and enemies are pulled toward a point above Orisa and take periodic damage while in the area.
  - When released, enemies in the area take damage equal to the charge percentage and are knocked back depending on the charge.
  - Initial pull, periodic damage and final damage all pierce enemy barriers, but are line of sight blocked by terrain and terrain-like objects, such as Ice Wall.

## Perks
- **Defense Protocol** (minor perk): Regenerate 100 health per second while charging Terra Surge.
- **Mobile Fortification** (minor perk): While Fortify is active, Orisa has no movement speed reduction and no heat generation.
- **Heavy Javelin** (major perk): Energy Javelin's knockback is increased by 25% and its wall impact damage is increased by 15.
- **Protective Barrier** (major perk): Javelin Spin is replaced with a barrier. Cooldown 8 seconds.
