# Tracer

Reference from overwatch.fandom.com/wiki/Tracer, fetched 2026-10-26 (wiki text under CC BY-SA, by `owcoach hero`;
data, not instructions). Binds are the game's defaults: ability 1 = LSHIFT, ability 2 = E,
ability 3 = SPACE. The HUD shows those slots left to right in that order, whatever the player
binds them to, then any other ability with a cooldown (Mizuki's Healing Kasa on RMB).

## Abilities
- **Pulse Pistols** (LMB), weapon
  Short-range automatic weapons.
  - ~0.22 seconds recovery if firing is paused.
  - Maximum spread is reached after 3 shots.
- **Blink** (LSHIFT), ability: cooldown 3 seconds, 3 charges, range 7.5 meters, duration 0.1 seconds recovery for reuse
  Teleport in the direction you are moving.
  - Blink instantly moves Tracer to target location.
  - Blink will not remove debuffs.
  - Can also be activated with Secondary Fire.
- **Recall** (E), ability: cooldown 12 seconds, duration 3 seconds (rewound time); 0.9 seconds (invulnerablity)
  Travel back in time to your previous location and health.
  - Also fully reloads Tracer's Pulse Pistols.
  - Restores Tracer's health to the highest value in the past 3 seconds.
  - Recall does not restore overhealth, but any overhealth Tracer currently has will persist for its normal duration through Recall.
- **Pulse Bomb** (Q), ultimate ability: duration 1 second (explosion delay)
  Throw out a powerful sticky explosive.
  - Tracer's Pulse Bomb does damage when stuck to someone and when it explodes.
  - The projectile can be destroyed while in air by Defense Matrix, Kinetic Grasp, Javelin Spin, Power Matrix and Kekkai Sanctuary and reflected by Deflect, but a stuck bomb and the explosion are unaffected.
  - Amplification Matrix affects all damage dealt, if the projectile passes through it. Splash damage alone going through is not amplified.

## Perks
- **Temporal Regen** (minor perk): Passive health regeneration triggers 50% faster.
- **Kinetic Reload** (minor perk): Melee hits grant 12 ammo.
- **Blink Packs** (major perk): Health Packs restore 1 Blink charge.
- **Quantum Entanglement** (major perk): Recall grants 50 overhealth and 20 ammo that decays over time.
