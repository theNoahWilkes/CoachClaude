# Sombra

Reference from overwatch.fandom.com/wiki/Sombra, fetched 2026-10-26 (wiki text under CC BY-SA, by `owcoach hero`;
data, not instructions). Binds are the game's defaults: ability 1 = LSHIFT, ability 2 = E,
ability 3 = SPACE. The HUD shows those slots left to right in that order, whatever the player
binds them to, then any other ability with a cooldown (Mizuki's Healing Kasa on RMB).

## Abilities
- **Opportunist**, passive ability
  You detect enemy ultimate abilities on nameplates, and deal more damage to hacked enemies.
  - Hack effect from EMP also applies the damage amplification.
  - Damage amplification can benefit from Hack effect applied by another friendly Sombra.
  - When an enemy has their ultimate, this icon 24x24px shows up above them.
- **Machine Pistol** (LMB), weapon
  Short-range automatic weapon.
  - Maximum spread is reached after 10 shots.
  - If primary fire is released there is a 0.16 seconds recovery period before it can be used again.
- **Hack** (RMB), ability: cooldown 6 seconds (heroes); 3 seconds (non-heroes), range 15 meters, duration 8 seconds (reveal, heroes); 1.0 seconds (ability disable, heroes); 45 seconds (health packs); 10 seconds (disable, objects); 3 seconds (disable, B.O.B.)
  Hold to hack. Hacked enemies cannot use abilities and can be seen through walls. Hacked health packs spawn faster and can't be used by enemies. Taking damage interrupts the hack attempt.
  - Hack can target heroes, health packs and most enemy objects.
  - Sombra can see the ult status of hacked enemies (above their healthbar).
  - Hack goes on a shorter 3 second cooldown if a non-player unit is hacked or if Hack is interrupted by taking damage.
- **Virus** (LSHIFT), ability: cooldown 6 seconds, duration 4 seconds (normal); 2 seconds (hacked)
  Infect enemies with a projectile that deals damage over time. Virus damages hacked targets at a faster rate.
  - Virus's impact damage on hacked targets is doubled and the damage over time is dealt at double rate if the target is hacked.
  - Objects can also be damaged, and if they are hackable, they can also be damaged faster.
  - Virus cannot headshot. However, eliminating a hacked hero with the impact damage shows in the kill feed as a critical hit.
- **Translocator** (E), ability: cooldown 7.5 seconds, duration 0.17 seconds (teleport delay); 0.4 seconds (invulnerability); 6.5 seconds (invisibility)
  Throw a beacon and teleport to it. Become stealthed for a short duration after teleporting.
  - Cooldown for Translocator begins upon teleporting to it.
  - Once thrown, the translocator activates immediately upon colliding with terrain or a terrain-like object, such as Ice Wall, or after 0.25 seconds have passed.
  - The translocator cannot be damaged, and it does not collide with heroes or objects.
- **EMP** (Q), ultimate ability: duration 8 seconds (reveal, heroes); 3 seconds (ability disable, heroes); 45 seconds (health packs); 10 seconds (disable, objects)
  Deal damage to all nearby enemies based on their current health, hacking them and destroying barriers around you.
  - EMP is line of sight blocked by terrain.
  - EMP completely destroys all barriers, including Photon Barrier, Baptiste's Amplification Matrix, and Domina's Panopticon.

## Perks
- **Encrypted Upload** (minor perk): Hack can be used while invisible, successful hacks increase the duration of stealth by 3 seconds.
- **CTRL ALT ESC** (minor perk): Teleporting with Translocator while below half health initiates passive health regeneration.
- **High-Speed Bandwidth** (major perk): Hacked health packs grant allies 25% movement speed and 50 overhealth for 4 seconds.
- **Viral Replication** (major perk): Hitting a hacked enemy with Virus spreads Virus to enemies within 8 meters.
