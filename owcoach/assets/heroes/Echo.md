# Echo

Reference from overwatch.fandom.com/wiki/Echo, fetched 2026-10-26 (wiki text under CC BY-SA, by `owcoach hero`;
data, not instructions). Binds are the game's defaults: ability 1 = LSHIFT, ability 2 = E,
ability 3 = SPACE. The HUD shows those slots left to right in that order, whatever the player
binds them to, then any other ability with a cooldown (Mizuki's Healing Kasa on RMB).

## Abilities
- **Glide** (SPACE), passive ability
  Hold jump to glide while falling.
  - Used by holding the Jump button.
  - Glide's movement speed buff does not count towards global movement speed buff cap.
- **Tri-Shot** (LMB), weapon
  Fires 3 shots at once, in a triangle pattern.
  - Damage per second: while firing ( overall w/reload)
- **Sticky Bombs** (RMB), ability: cooldown 6 seconds, duration 1 second (explosion delay)
  Fire a volley of sticky bombs that detonate after a delay.
  - Deals up to 180 single target damage if all bombs are stuck.
  - The projectile can be destroyed while in air by Defense Matrix, Kinetic Grasp, Javelin Spin, Power Matrix and Kekkai Sanctuary and reflected by Deflect, but a stuck bomb and the explosion are unaffected.
  - Amplification Matrix affects all damage dealt, if the projectile passes through it. Splash damage alone going through is not amplified.
- **Flight** (LSHIFT), ability: cooldown 6 seconds, duration 0.2 seconds (min); 3 seconds (max)
  Fly forward quickly and then free-fly briefly.
  - Reveals enemy health bars without having dealt damage to them.
  - Being stunned, knocked down or mobility locked prevent the ability from being activated and make Echo temporarily unable to fly, but do not completely end the ability. Only being hacked completely interrupts the ability.
  - Can be manually cancelled by pressing the ability key (default: ) again.
- **Focusing Beam** (E), ability: cooldown 8 seconds, range 16 meters, duration 0.24 seconds (min); 2 seconds (max)
  Channel a beam for a few seconds. Deals very high damage to targets under half health.
  - Deals 2.33 times more damage if she is targeting an enemy whose HP is 50% of their maximum or lower.
  - Can be manually cancelled by pressing the ability key again.
  - Focusing Beam is a channeled ability; Echo cannot fire or use other abilities while it's active, and it is interrupted if Echo is stunned, knocked down or hacked.
- **Duplicate** (Q), ultimate ability: range 40 meters, duration 15 seconds (transformation); 1.3 seconds (invulnerability, casting); 1 second (invulnerability, ending)
  Become a copy of the targeted hero. They cannot swap heroes for the duration.
  - Cannot target another Echo (even if that Echo is duplicating another hero) or non-playable characters, like B.O.B. or Training Bots.
  - If Echo targets a D.Va pilot, Echo will still duplicate the mech.
  - Echo's HP instantly becomes the full base HP of her target, however this caps at 350 combined health.

## Perks
- **Focused Rush** (minor perk): Focused Beam's range is increase by 6 meters and movement speed is increase by 20% when active.
- **Partial Scan** (minor perk): Duplicate starts with 30% of its ultimate charge.
- **Full Salvo** (major perk): Sticky Bombs fires 2 additional projectiles.
- **High Beams** (major perk): Focusing Beam eliminations reset Flight's cooldown.
