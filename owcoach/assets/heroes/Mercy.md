# Mercy

Reference from overwatch.fandom.com/wiki/Mercy, fetched 2026-10-26 (wiki text under CC BY-SA, by `owcoach hero`;
data, not instructions). Binds are the game's defaults: ability 1 = LSHIFT, ability 2 = E,
ability 3 = SPACE. The HUD shows those slots left to right in that order, whatever the player
binds them to, then any other ability with a cooldown (Mizuki's Healing Kasa on RMB).

## Abilities
- **Swift Recovery**, passive ability
  Begin regenerating health more quickly.
  - Reduces the time before passive health regeneration starts from 6 to 3 seconds after taking damage.
- **Angelic Descent** (SPACE), passive ability: duration Until cancelled
  Fall very slowly.
  - Can be activated also by holding Crouch key.
- **Caduceus Staff** (LMB), weapon: range 15 meters
  primary fire Hold to heal an ally.
  - Creates a beam that locks on to a nearby ally. Ignores barriers and will linger for 1.3 seconds if the target breaks line of sight or moves out of range. Mercy does not need to look at her target to maintain connection.
- **Caduceus Staff Alt Fire** (RMB), weapon: range 15 meters
  secondary fire Hold to increase an ally's damage inflicted.
  - In order to amplify a 's damage, Mercy must be boosting the hero when the projectile was fired. After that, it will be boosted even if Mercy stops boosting when it lands.
  - Damage over time abilities, such as Carnage and Incendiary Chaingun, will have their damage amplified if Mercy is boosting the hero whilst the damage is being applied to the enemy. After which, the damage will still be b
  - Mercy gains ultimate charge equal to the damage that she amplifies, except from self-damage.
- **Caduceus Blaster** (LMB), weapon
  Automatic weapon.
  - Damage per second = 100 while firing (78 overall w/reload)
  - The blaster is automatically reloaded if not equipped for 1.4 seconds.
  - Upon switching weapon, there is 0.51 second recovery period where Mercy cannot use her weapon.
- **Guardian Angel** (LSHIFT), ability: cooldown 1.5 seconds, range 30 meters
  Fly towards an ally. While in flight, Jump launches you forward, and Crouch launches you upward.
  - Can be used on both living and dead allies.
  - While flying, Guardian Angel be cancelled mid-flight by pressing the hotkey once more or extended through a Jump or Crouch input:
  - Extending Guardian Angel with a Jump or Crouch input sets its duration to 1 seconds. The extension cannot be canceled.
- **Resurrect** (E), ability: cooldown 30 seconds, range 5.50 meters
  Revive a dead teammate.
  - Eliminated allies leave souls, appearing as glowing yellow balls, at the location of their death. Targeting a soul with Resurrect starts the cast.
  - Resurrect is a channeled ability. Mercy cannot use other abilities while channeling and the cast is interrupted, immediately starting the cooldown without resurrecting, if Mercy is stunned, knocked down or hacked.
  - Once Resurrect starts casting, line of sight does not need to be maintained. However, the cast is interrupted if Mercy moves more than 7 meters away from her target while channeling.
- **Flash Heal** (R), ability: cooldown 12 seconds
  Heal Caduceus Staff's active target, healing is doubled on allies below half health.
  - Flash Heal can be used regardless of if Caduceus Staff is healing or boosting damage.
- **Valkyrie** (Q), ultimate ability: range 10 meters (chain healing); +100% range (Guardian Angel, Caduceus Staff), duration 15 seconds
  Gain the ability to fly. Abilities are enhanced.
  - Caduceus Staff's beam branches out from the beam target to nearby allies within line of sight (of the beam target).
  - Global self-healing passive will be always active for the duration of Valkyrie.
  - Mercy gains vision of enemy health bars if they have taken damage (even if Mercy did not deal the damage herself).

## Perks
- **Angelic Resurrection** (minor perk): Mercy gains 100 overhealth after casting Resurrect.
- **Winged Reach** (minor perk): Guardian Angel's range is increased by 30%.
- **Chain Boost** (major perk): Caduceus Staff's damdge boost is increased by 5% and links to a second nearby ally.
- **Double Dose** (major perk): Flash Heal gains an additional charge but its base healing is reduced by 10.
