- **Restorative Stream** (RMB), ability: range 20 meters, duration 3 seconds (LoS timeout, passive); 1 second (LoS timeout, manual); 2 seconds (recharge delay)
  Place a passive healing stream on an ally. Hold on the target to manually heal at an improved rate, consuming resource.
  - Manual healing stacks with passive healing, for a total of 75 health per second.
  - Resource starts regenerating at a rate of 15% per second after 2 seconds of not being used.
  - Targeting another ally with the beam will immediately move the passive stream to them.
- **Rushing Torrent** (LSHIFT), ability: cooldown 8 seconds, range 3.7 meters (vertical), duration 3.5 seconds
  Ride water to move faster and jump higher.
  - Grants a short burst of movement speed when activated.
  - Wuyang has less friction with the ground while using Rushing Torrent.
  - Wuyang cannot crouch while Rushing Torrent is active.
- **Guardian Wave** (E), ability: cooldown 14 seconds, range 30 meters, duration 3 seconds (healing amplification)
  Send a water wave forward that increases the amount of healing received by allies and knocks back enemies.
  - Guardian Wave requires contact with a walkable surface to be cast. If Wuyang is or becomes airborne during the cast, he will remain locked in the cast animation until he makes contact with a valid surface.
  - The wave travels along the ground and can go up and down slopes and small elevations.
  - Damage and knockback strength are reduced based on distance to target.
- **Tidal Blast** (Q), ultimate ability: range 35 meters, duration 3.5 seconds (explosion delay); 1.5 seconds (knockdown)
  Protect an ally or yourself with water that detonates after a short duration. The explosion knocks down enemies and massively heals the host target.
  - A confirmation prompt is given upon activation, allowing Wuyang to choose whether to target an ally (Primary Fire) or himself (Secondary Fire). Pressing ultimate ability key again cancels the targeting.
  - The target ally (or Wuyang) is granted overhealth and movement speed while the explosion winds up. Upon explosion, the overhealth and speed boost are removed and the ally is healed.
  - The target is not immune to negative effects while carrying Tidal Blast.

## Perks
- **Overflow** (minor perk): Gain 10 ammo and 50% healing resource when Rushing Torrent is activated.
- **Balance** (minor perk): When you deal damage with water orbs, increase Restorative Stream's passive healing by 30% for 2 seconds.
- **Ebb and Flow** (major perk): Guardian Wave rewinds to its starting location.
- **Falling Rain** (major perk): Simultaneously control 3 water orbs that deal 60% decreased damage and have have 25% decreased empowered explosion radius.
