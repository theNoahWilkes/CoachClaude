# D.Va

Reference from overwatch.fandom.com/wiki/D.Va, fetched 2026-10-26 (wiki text under CC BY-SA, by `owcoach hero`;
data, not instructions). Binds are the game's defaults: ability 1 = LSHIFT, ability 2 = E,
ability 3 = SPACE. The HUD shows those slots left to right in that order, whatever the player
binds them to, then any other ability with a cooldown (Mizuki's Healing Kasa on RMB).

## Abilities
- **Eject!**, passive ability
  Eject out of your mech when it's destroyed.
  - D.Va can use Self-Destruct while ejecting.
  - D.Va is invulnerable while ejecting for 1.8 seconds.
  - If D.Va falls off the map while still in her mech, she will be instantly eliminated.
- **Fusion Cannons** (LMB), weapon (mech)
  Automatic short-range spread weapons.
  - Damage per second: 146 while firing
  - No movement speed penalty while Boosters are active.
- **Light Gun** (LMB), weapon (pilot)
  Automatic weapon.
- **Defense Matrix** (RMB), ability (mech): cooldown 1 second, range 10.3 meters, duration 0.5 seconds (min); 3 seconds (max); 0.75 seconds (energy regen delay)
  Block projectiles in an area in front of you.
  - D.Va cannot fire Fusion Cannons or use quick melee while channeling Defense Matrix.
  - Can be used simultaneously with Boosters and Micro Missiles.
  - Defense Matrix has a resource meter which depletes while it is active and recharges when not in use.
- **Boosters** (LSHIFT), ability (mech): cooldown 4 seconds, range 24 meters, duration 0.4 seconds (min); 2 seconds (max)
  Fly in the direction you are facing.
  - Can be used simultaneously with Fusion Cannons, quick melee, Micro Missiles, and/or Defense Matrix.
  - Does not affect Fusion Cannons or Micro Missiles accuracy. Although reticles with the 'show accuracy' option enabled suggest a spread increase when using the Boosters, it is a purely cosmetic change.
  - Boosters movement speed is unaffected by gravity, but is affected by slowing effects.
- **Micro Missiles** (E), ability (mech): cooldown 7 seconds
  Launch a volley of explosive rockets.
  - Deals up to a total of 135 damage with direct hits.
  - Cannot be manually cancelled.
  - If the ability is interrupted before the end of the casting time, the cooldown will not start.
- **Self-Destruct** (Q), ultimate ability (mech): duration 3 seconds
  Eject and overload your mech, causing it to explode after a short time.
  - Enemies hit by the explosion are also knocked back.
  - If Self-Destruct was activated while Boosters were on, they will remain active for their remaining duration.
  - Automatically reloads the Light Gun.
- **Call Mech** (Q), ultimate ability (pilot): range 1.5 meters
  Call down a new mech.
  - Enemies hit by Call Mech take damage and are knocked back.
  - Becomes completely charged after Self-Destruct detonates, or if D.Va is Resurrect.
  - Has to be grounded to activate.

## Perks
- **Bunny Power** (minor perk): Eject grants 75 temporary overhealth and Call Mech's damage radius is increased by 50%.
- **Extended Boosters** (minor perk): Hitting an enemy with Boosters deals 40% increased damage and extends the duration by 0.5 seconds.
- **Shield System** (major perk): Convert 100 health to shields. Defense Matrix restores shields based on 25% of its damage absorbed.
- **Precision Fusion** (major perk): Press Reload to reduce Fusion Cannons' Spread by 75% for 3 seconds. Cooldown 8 seconds.
