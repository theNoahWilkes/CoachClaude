# Illari

Reference from overwatch.fandom.com/wiki/Illari, fetched 2026-10-26 (wiki text under CC BY-SA, by `owcoach hero`;
data, not instructions). Binds are the game's defaults: ability 1 = LSHIFT, ability 2 = E,
ability 3 = SPACE. The HUD shows those slots left to right in that order, whatever the player
binds them to, then any other ability with a cooldown (Mizuki's Healing Kasa on RMB).

## Abilities
- **Solar Rifle** (LMB), weapon: duration 0.65 seconds (charge time); 0.25 seconds (recovery)
  Long-range auto charging rifle.
  - Damage per second (max charge): while firing ( overall w/ reload)
  - Damage per second (min charge): while firing ( overall w/ reload)
  - Using secondary fire, quick melee or abilities do not interrupt charging.
- **Solar Rifle Alt Fire** (RMB), weapon: range 20 meters, duration 0.6 seconds (energy regen delay)
  Medium range healing beam that consumes solar energy.
  - Can be fired continuously for 3 seconds before running out of energy.
  - Takes 2.3 seconds to recharge to full once regeneration starts.
  - If completely ran out of energy, it takes an additional 0.4 seconds before energy starts recharging and secondary fire cannot be used again until the energy is fully recharged.
- **Outburst** (LSHIFT), ability: cooldown 8 seconds
  Launches you in the direction you are moving. Knocking back enemies. Hold jump to go higher.
  - Knockback: 8 meters
- **Healing Pylon** (E), ability: cooldown 7 seconds (default); 14 seconds (under attack), range 15 meters, duration Until destroyed; 1.3 seconds (build time)
  Deploy a pylon that heals allies.
  - Heal per second: 50 (allies), 25 (self)
  - Healing prioritizes the hero with the lowest health percentage within the pylon's range.
  - Cooldown starts immediately upon activation.
- **Captive Sun** (Q), ultimate ability: duration 5.2 seconds (flight); 6 seconds (Sunstruck debuff); 0.8 seconds (explosion delay)
  Fire an explosive ball of solar energy. Enemies hit are slowed and explode after taking significant damage.
  - Captive Sun is a channeled ability; it is interrupted by becoming stunned, knocked down or hacked, including subsidiary effects, consuming the ultimate charge.
  - Launches Illari into the air granting her free flight for the duration and her next shot is replaced by solar energy ball.
  - If a marked enemy receives total of 90 damage after reductions or is killed, they will become "SUNSTRUCK", causing the mark to explode after 0.8 seconds dealing damage in an area.

## Perks
- **Rapid Construction** (minor perk): Healing Pylon builds 300% faster and its cooldown is reduced by 2 seconds. Cooldown 7 → 5 seconds (default); 14 → 12 seconds (under attack).
- **Summer Solstice** (minor perk): Activating Captive Sun grants Illari 3 extra seconds of flight, 20% increased flight speed, and 20% increased attack speed.
- **Solar Flare** (major perk): Press Primary Fire while using Solar Rifle's healing beam to heal all allies in front of Illari for 100 healing. Cooldown 10 seconds.
- **Sunburn** (major perk): Outburst ignites enemies, dealing an additional 50 damage over 3 seconds.
