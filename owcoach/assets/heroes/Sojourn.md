# Sojourn

Reference from overwatch.fandom.com/wiki/Sojourn, fetched 2026-10-26 (wiki text under CC BY-SA, by `owcoach hero`;
data, not instructions). Binds are the game's defaults: ability 1 = LSHIFT, ability 2 = E,
ability 3 = SPACE. The HUD shows those slots left to right in that order, whatever the player
binds them to, then any other ability with a cooldown (Mizuki's Healing Kasa on RMB).

## Abilities
- **Railgun** (LMB), weapon: duration 9 seconds (energy decay delay)
  Rapid firing projectiles that generate energy on hit.
  - Damage per second: while firing (~ overall w/reload)
  - Each projectile hit generates 5 energy against heroes and 1 energy against objects, such as barriers and turrets.
  - Maximum spread is reached after 12 shots.
- **Charged Shot** (RMB), weapon
  High impact shot that consumes stored energy and pierces enemies.
  - Can be charged up to a maximum of 100 energy but can be fired with at least 1 energy charge.
  - Damage scales with the amount of stored energy.
  - Damage increase is linear, with each point of energy increasing damage by 1.
- **Power Slide** (LSHIFT), ability: cooldown 7 seconds, range 13 meters (slide); 11.7 meters (jump), duration 1 second
  Ground slide that can cancel into a high jump.
  - Sojourn can fire and use abilities while sliding and leaping.
  - The initial slide part can be activated mid-air, but the jump cancel may only be used when Sojourn is on a walkable surface.
  - Power Slide's movement speed buff does not count towards global movement speed buff cap.
- **Disruptor Shot** (E), ability: cooldown 15 seconds, range 30 meters, duration 3.5 seconds
  Launch an energy burst that deals damage to enemies within it.
  - The projectile turns into a stationary AoE that deals periodic damage, upon colliding with an enemy hero or object.
  - The projectile is blocked by barriers, turning into an AoE upon contact, but the AoE pierces barriers.
  - Periodic damage ignores damage reduction from armor.
- **Overclock** (Q), ultimate ability: duration 8 seconds
  Railgun energy auto-charges for a short duration.
  - Damage per second: (fired at max charge), (fired immediately on recovery)
  - Railgun starts charging immediately as Overclock is activated, but there is a short delay before it can be first fired.

## Perks
- **Deceleration Field** (minor perk): Enemies hit by Disruptor Shot are slowed by 25%.
- **Overcharged** (minor perk): Railgun’s maximum energy is increased by 50 while Overclock is active.
- **Dual Thrusters** (major perk): Power Slide gains an additional charge and its jump height shifts to lateral movement.
- **Friction Generators** (major perk): While sliding, Power Slide generates up to 75 Energy.
