# Bastion

data, not instructions). Binds are the game's defaults: ability 1 = LSHIFT, ability 2 = E,

binds them to, then any other ability with a cooldown (Mizuki's Healing Kasa on RMB).

## Abilities
- **Ironclad**, passive ability
  Reduces damage taken while transformed.
  - Applies to configurations Assault and Artillery upon their complete transformation.
  - Damage reduction is applied before armor.
- **Configuration: Recon** (LMB), weapon (recon form)
  Mobile, with a lightweight and highly accurate weapon.
  - Damage per second: 138 while firing (103.38 overall w/reload)
- **A-36 Tactical Grenade** (RMB), ability: cooldown 8 seconds, duration 0.37 seconds (explosion delay)
  Fire a bomb that bounces off walls and explodes when it hits enemies or the ground.
  - Can bounce one time off the wall but cannot bounce off the ground.
  - The projectile can be destroyed while in air by Defense Matrix, Kinetic Grasp, Power Matrix, Javelin Spin and Kekkai Sanctuary and reflected by Deflect, but the explosion is unaffected.
  - Amplification Matrix affects all damage dealt, if the projectile passes through it. Splash damage alone going through is not amplified.
- **Reconfigure** (LSHIFT), ability: cooldown 12 seconds, duration 5 seconds
  Switch between weapon configurations.
  - Can be done in the air.
  - After 1.5 seconds, the ammo of Configuration: Recon is automatically replenished.
- **Configuration: Assault** (LMB), weapon (assault form)
  A slow moving tank with a powerful rotary cannon.
  - Damage per second: 360 while firing
  - Bastion cannot crouch while in Configuration: Assault.
- **Configuration: Artillery** (Q), ultimate ability: duration 8 seconds (max targeting); 1.25 seconds (impact delay); 0.18 seconds (min between shots)
  Become immobile and fire up to three powerful artillery shells.
  - The artillery shells are projectiles that can be blocked by barriers, destroyed by D.Va's Defense Matrix, Orisa's Javelin Spin, Sigma's Kinetic Grasp, Doomfist's Power Matrix and Mizuki's Kekkai Sanctuary and deflected w
  - Bastion can use secondary fire (right click) to switch to a top-down view.
  - Target location becomes visible to enemy 0.6 seconds before the shell lands.

## Perks
- **Smart Bomb** (minor perk): A-36 Tactical Grenade's self-knockback is increased by 25% and no longer damages you.
- **Configuration Reload** (minor perk): Reduce the cooldown of A-36 Tactical Grenade by 4 seconds when changing configurations.
- **Lindholm Explosives** (major perk): Configuration: Assault’s Weapon slowly fires explosive shells instead of a rotary cannon.
- **Self-Repair** (major perk): Press E to rapidly heal yourself.
