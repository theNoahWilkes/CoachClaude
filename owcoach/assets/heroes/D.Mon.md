# D.Mon

Reference from overwatch.fandom.com/wiki/D.Mon, fetched 2026-10-26 (wiki text under CC BY-SA, by `owcoach hero`;
data, not instructions). Binds are the game's defaults: ability 1 = LSHIFT, ability 2 = E,
ability 3 = SPACE. The HUD shows those slots left to right in that order, whatever the player
binds them to, then any other ability with a cooldown (Mizuki's Healing Kasa on RMB).

## Abilities
- **Eject! (D.Mon)**, passive ability
  Eject out of your mech when it is destroyed
  - If D.Mon falls off the map while still in her mech, she will be instantly eliminated.
  - D.Mon is invulnerable while ejecting for approximately 1.4 seconds.
  - D.Mon is unable to crouch while inside her mech.
- **Plasma Saber** (LMB), weapon (mech): range 4 meters
  Fast energy melee weapon
  - Damage per second: 91.2 while swinging
- **Portable Fusion Repeater** (LMB), weapon (pilot)
  Rapid fire machine gun
  - Damage per second: 93.75 while firing
- **Power Barrier** (RMB), ability (mech): cooldown 5 seconds (Destroyed)
  Hold a frontal energy barrier
  - Barrier begins regenerating at a rate of 65 shields per second after it is inactive for 2s.
- **Propulsors** (LSHIFT), ability (mech): cooldown 1 second, duration 4s max
  Consume fuel to surge in any horizontal direction
  - Can be used simultaneously with Power Barrier.
- **Fusion Repeater** (E), ability (mech): cooldown 4 seconds, duration 4 seconds (max)
  Activate a rapid-fire machine gun
  - Damage per second: ~144 while firing
  - Can be cancelled early.
  - Cooldown starts when ability ends.
- **Surging Strike** (LMB), ability (mech): cooldown 6 seconds, range 5 meters (AoE) ; 10 meters (Dash)
  While Power Barrier is deployed, strike forward to damage and push enemies back
  - Power Barrier is no longer deployed when using Surging Strike.
- **Limit Break** (Q), ultimate ability (mech): range 20m, duration 3s (overhealth decay delay); 5s (Dmg amp duration)
  Unleash a sweeping slash that grants self overhealth and amplifies damage taken by enemies
  - Slash can be aimed omni-directionally.
  - Can be interrupted by crowd control during the wind-up of the slash, consuming the ultimate charge without the slash being fired.
- **Call Mech (D.Mon)** (Q), ultimate ability (pilot): duration 0.95 seconds (tranformation)
  Reassemble mech
  - Enemies hit by Call Mech take damage and are knocked back.
  - Unlike D.Va's Call Mech, this ability can be cast in mid-air.
  - Becomes completely charged if D.Mon is Resurrect.

## Perks
- **Beast Within** (minor perk): Plasma Saber hits heal Power Shield by 40 health
- **MEKA Mobility** (minor perk): While holding Power Shield, Propulsors fuel cost is reduced by 30%
- **Overstrike** (major perk): Surging Strike gains 150% lifesteal
- **Focused Fusion** (major perk): Fusion Repeater fires stronger shots with no spread at a slower rate
