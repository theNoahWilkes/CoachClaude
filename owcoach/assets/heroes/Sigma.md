# Sigma

Reference from overwatch.fandom.com/wiki/Sigma, fetched 2026-10-26 (wiki text under CC BY-SA, by `owcoach hero`;
data, not instructions). Binds are the game's defaults: ability 1 = LSHIFT, ability 2 = E,
ability 3 = SPACE. The HUD shows those slots left to right in that order, whatever the player
binds them to, then any other ability with a cooldown (Mizuki's Healing Kasa on RMB).

## Abilities
- **Hyperspheres** (LMB), weapon: range 21 meters
  Launch two charges which implode after a short duration, dealing damage in an area.
  - Damage per second: ~80.4 while firing
  - Bounces off walls, floors, and ceilings. Hitting a hero or an object causes the charges to implode.
  - The charges automatically implode after travelling 22 meters along the trajectory. However, the maximum reach of Hyperspheres is slightly less than 22 meters due to the fact that the charges also travel downwards.
- **Experimental Barrier** (RMB), ability: cooldown 1 second (recalled); 5 seconds (destroyed), range ∞, duration Until recalled or destroyed
  Hold to propel a floating barrier; release to stop. Press again to recall the barrier to you.
  - On default settings, the barrier moves forward by holding down the ability key and stops when released.
  - Barrier health starts to regenerate at a rate of 79.3 per second after being recalled for 2 seconds.
  - Deploying the barrier is a channeled action; use of fire and abilities is limited, and being stunned or knocked down or hacked, including their subsidiary effects, interrupts the cast.
- **Kinetic Grasp** (LSHIFT), ability: cooldown 12 seconds, range 3 meters, duration 2 seconds
  Absorb projectiles in front of you and convert them into extra health.
  - Sigma cannot use primary fire or deploy Experimental Barrier while it's active.
  - Experimental Barrier can be retracted without interrupting the ability.
  - Accretion or Gravitic Flux can be activated to cancel Kinetic Grasp.
- **Accretion** (E), ability: cooldown 10 seconds, duration 1.1 seconds (knockdown)
  Gather a mass of debris and throw it at an enemy to knock them down.
  - Accretion is a channeled ability; the use of primary fire and other abilities is limited, and the ability is interrupted if Sigma is stunned, knocked down or hacked, including their subsidiary effects.
  - Sigma cannot crouch while casting Accretion.
  - Sigma can damage himself with the splash damage if he is too close to the rock's impact point.
- **Gravitic Flux** (Q), ultimate ability: range 35 meters, duration 5 seconds (targeting); 1 second (activation delay); 2.5 seconds (lift); 0.6 seconds (high gravity)
  Manipulate gravity to lift enemies into the air and slam them back down.
  - Gravitic Flux's targeting area is cylindrical: Its bottom is a flat 8m radius circle situated 5.5m below the targeted (ground) location and the upper limit of the area is defined by a 15.5m radius sphere centered on this
  - Targeting is based off line-of-sight from the dark center circle of the selected area. Any enemies who can block line of sight from the center, such as by hiding behind a wall, will not be affected by the initial lift.
  - The selected area is visible to the enemy team briefly before the effect activates.

## Perks
- **Kinetic Cycle** (minor perk): Absorbing projectiles with Kinetic Grasp also reduces Accretion's cooldown.
- **Hyper Regeneration** (minor perk): 30% of Hyperspheres' damage restores Experimental Barrier's Health.
- **Hyper Strike** (major perk): Every 5 direct hits with Hyperspheres, your next successful Quick Melee levitates and knocks away enemies.
- **Levitation** (major perk): Activate and hold Double Jump to briefly levitate upward.
