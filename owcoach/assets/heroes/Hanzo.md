# Hanzo

Reference from overwatch.fandom.com/wiki/Hanzo, fetched 2026-10-26 (wiki text under CC BY-SA, by `owcoach hero`;
data, not instructions). Binds are the game's defaults: ability 1 = LSHIFT, ability 2 = E,
ability 3 = SPACE. The HUD shows those slots left to right in that order, whatever the player
binds them to, then any other ability with a cooldown (Mizuki's Healing Kasa on RMB).

## Abilities
- **Wall Climb** (SPACE), passive ability: duration Up to 1 second
  Jump at walls to climb up them.
  - Wall Climb ends when the player stops holding the button, the wall ends or the duration is up. It cannot then be used again until the player has landed on a walkable surface.
  - Hanzo will grab and mantle ledges if the input is held while facing a ledge of a walkable surface.
  - Can reach up to 8.8m height (10m with Lunge) due to momentum being kept after Wall Climb ends.
- **Storm Bow** (LMB), weapon
  Hold to charge then release to launch arrows further.
  - Damage per second: at full charge, at min charge
  - Fire recovery stops while using melee.
  - The arrow can deal damage no earlier than 0.032 second after being launched.
- **Sonic Arrow** (LSHIFT), ability: cooldown 12 seconds, duration 6 seconds
  Reveals enemies for a short time upon impact.
  - Pressing the ability key turns the next arrow from Storm Bow or Storm Arrows into a Sonic Arrow. This does not change the speed, damage or projectile radius of the arrow. Pressing the key again before firing changes it b
  - The cooldown starts when the arrow is fired.
  - If the arrow becomes stuck on a moving target (e.g. an enemy hero), the effect follows.
- **Storm Arrows** (E), ability: cooldown 8 seconds, duration Up to 5 seconds
  The next 5 arrows ricochet and fire instantly at reduced damage.
  - Damage per second: ~300 while firing
  - Cooldown starts when the ability ends.
  - Can be manually cancelled by pressing the ability key again.
- **Lunge** (SPACE), ability: cooldown 4 seconds, range 7.85 meters
  Double Jump.
- **Dragonstrike** (Q), ultimate ability: range 10 meters (initial arrow); Infinite (dragons), duration 0.5 seconds (initial arrow)
  Launch a deadly Dragon Spirit that devastates enemies it passes through.
  - Hanzo can gain ultimate charge while Dragonstrike is active, but the damage dealt by it does not generate any charge.
  - The initial arrow can headshot with the default 2x multiplier.
  - The initial arrow and the AoE both pierce enemies, barriers and other objects, and both cannot be blocked by walls.

## Perks
- **Sonic Disruption** (minor perk): Sonic Arrow hacks nearby Health Packs for 30 seconds.
- **Dragon Fury** (major perk): After hitting an enemy with Primary Fire, gain 20% attack speed for 1.5 seconds.
- **Frost Arrow** (major perk): Press Reload to ready an explosive frost arrow that slows enemies by 35% for 2 seconds. Cooldown 14 seconds.
- **Scatter Arrows** (major perk): On first ricochet, Storm Arrows split into 5 shots dealing 33% damage and bounce 1 extra time.
