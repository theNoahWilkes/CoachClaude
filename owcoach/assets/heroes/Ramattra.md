# Ramattra

Reference from overwatch.fandom.com/wiki/Ramattra, fetched 2026-10-26 (wiki text under CC BY-SA, by `owcoach hero`;
data, not instructions). Binds are the game's defaults: ability 1 = LSHIFT, ability 2 = E,
ability 3 = SPACE. The HUD shows those slots left to right in that order, whatever the player
binds them to, then any other ability with a cooldown (Mizuki's Healing Kasa on RMB).

## Abilities
- **Void Accelerator** (LMB), weapon (omnic form)
  Fire a stream of projectiles.
  - Damage per second: 125 (100 overall w/ reload)
  - Exclusive to Omnic form.
  - No damage fall-off
- **Void Barrier** (RMB), ability (omnic form): cooldown 12 seconds, range 35 meters, duration 4 seconds
  Create a barrier at the targeted location.
  - Exclusive to Omnic form.
  - Hold to target. While targeting, a line on the floor is generated that shows the player where the barrier will be drawn when the button is released.
  - Ability is stationed wherever it's placed. So, if placed on top of a temporary or moving surface, like Ice Wall or the Payload, it will remain in midair, even after the surface expires or moves.
- **Ravenous Vortex** (E), ability (both forms): cooldown 11 seconds, duration 3 seconds
  Fire a sphere of energy that creates a slowing vortex on the ground, slowing and pulling down enemies. Re-activate to detonate early.
  - Available to both forms.
  - The projectile must hit the ground to activate.
  - The projectile is blocked by barriers, but the AoE is not.
- **Nemesis Form** (LSHIFT), ability: cooldown 8 seconds, duration 8 seconds
  Transform into Nemesis Form, changing your attacks and gaining bonus armor.
  - Grants Ramattra armor and movement speed and replaces Void Accelerator and Void Barrier with Pummel and Block.
  - Cooldown starts when the transformation ends.
  - Nemesis Form is not interrupted by crowd control.
- **Pummel** (LMB), weapon (nemesis form): range 10 meters
  Punch, creating a wave of piercing energy with every swing.
  - Damage per second:
  - Exclusive to Nemesis form.
  - Punches pierce enemies and barriers.
- **Block** (RMB), ability (nemesis form): cooldown 0.5 seconds, duration 0.2 seconds (min)
  Significantly reduces damage taken from the front and reduces movement speed.
  - Exclusive to Nemesis form.
  - Cooldown starts when blocking is stopped.
  - No maximum duration; only limited by the duration of Nemesis Form.
- **Annihilation** (Q), ultimate ability: duration 3 - 20 seconds
  Enter Nemesis Form and create a deadly swarm that lashes out at nearby enemies, persisting longer when damaging enemies.
  - Transforms into Nemesis form until the Ultimate ends.
  - Annihilation's tether damage by energy swarm is not interrupted by crowd control.
  - Timer ticks down at 85% reduced rate when any enemy Heroes are affected by Annihilation, resulting in a maximum duration of 20 seconds.

## Perks
- **Relentless Form** (minor perk): While Nemesis Form is active, eliminations extend the duration by 2 seconds. Half duration gained during Annihilation.
- **Prolonged Barrier** (minor perk): Void Barrier's duration and size is increased by 25%.
- **Nanite Repair** (major perk): Ramattra is healed for 75 health per second while within Ravenous Vortex.
- **Void Surge** (major perk): Void Accelerator periodically releases a burst of 6 additional projectiles during continuous fire.
