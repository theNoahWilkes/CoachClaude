# Mei

Reference from overwatch.fandom.com/wiki/Mei, fetched 2026-10-26 (wiki text under CC BY-SA, by `owcoach hero`;
data, not instructions). Binds are the game's defaults: ability 1 = LSHIFT, ability 2 = E,
ability 3 = SPACE. The HUD shows those slots left to right in that order, whatever the player
binds them to, then any other ability with a cooldown (Mizuki's Healing Kasa on RMB).

## Abilities
- **Endothermic Blaster** (LMB), weapon: range 12 meters, duration 1.3 seconds (slow)
  Short-range spray weapon that slows enemies.
  - Damage per second: 110 while firing (90.58 overall w/reload)
- **Icicle** (RMB), weapon
  Long-range icicle launcher.
  - Damage per second: 106.25 while firing (94.44 overall w/reload)
  - Recovery stops while using melee.
  - Even if there is less than 10 ammo left, the next icicle will be fired as normal before reloading.
- **Cryo-Freeze** (LSHIFT), ability: cooldown 12 seconds, duration 0.44 seconds min. duration; 4 seconds max. duration; 0.24 seconds recovery
  Become invulnerable and heal yourself.
  - Restores 15 ammo per second (max. 60).
  - The cooldown starts only after the effect wears off.
  - The ability can be cancelled prematurely.
- **Ice Wall** (E), ability: cooldown 12 seconds, range 25 meters, duration Up to 5 seconds; 0.5 seconds recovery
  Create a wall in front of you.
  - Can be manually destroyed by pressing the ability key again, including while dead.
  - Before casting, the Ice Wall's placement from the player's line of sight can be toggled from perpendicular to parallel by pressing the hotkey again.
  - The cooldown starts immediately, not when the wall destroyed.
- **Blizzard** (Q), ultimate ability: duration 4.25 seconds
  Launch a weather control drone that freezes enemies in a wide area.
  - Deals up to 85 damage over the duration.
  - The first tick results in a 50% progress towards the freeze (also inflicts a 50% slow effect), then it continues at a rate of 25% per second (also slows at a rate of 25% per second). (The slow effect is capped at 75%.) A
  - Using Endothermic Blaster's primary fire greatly speeds up the freezing process (and will allow targets to be frozen for a little more than 2.25 seconds).

## Perks
- **Skating Rink** (minor perk): Allies within Blizzard gain 25% increased movement speed and heal for 50 health per second.
- **Glacial Propulsion** (minor perk): Double jumping creates a small ice pillar that launches Mei into the air. Cooldown 10 seconds.
- **Deep Freeze** (major perk): Continuously hitting enemies with primary fire freezes them for a short time.
- **Cryo-Storm** (major perk): Cryo-Freeze slows and deals 70 damage per second to nearby enemies.
