# Cassidy

Reference from overwatch.fandom.com/wiki/Cassidy, fetched 2026-10-26 (wiki text under CC BY-SA, by `owcoach hero`;
data, not instructions). Binds are the game's defaults: ability 1 = LSHIFT, ability 2 = E,
ability 3 = SPACE. The HUD shows those slots left to right in that order, whatever the player
binds them to, then any other ability with a cooldown (Mizuki's Healing Kasa on RMB).

## Abilities
- **Peacekeeper** (LMB), weapon
  Accurate, powerful revolver.
  - Damage per second: 140 while firing (93.33 overall w/reload)
  - After the shot, the game raises the Cassidy camera (changes the facing angle) by 4 units, after which it starts lowering the character's camera to its previous value. As soon as this happens Cassidy can shoot again.
- **Fan the Hammer** (RMB), weapon
  Wildly fire remaining rounds.
  - Damage per second: while firing ( overall w/reload)
  - Each shot raises the Cassidy camera (changes the facing angle) by 2.5 units.
  - The first shot has no spread.
- **Combat Roll** (LSHIFT), ability: cooldown 5 seconds, range 6.22 meters, duration 0.4 seconds
  Roll in the direction you're moving to take reduced damage and reload.
  - Can be used while airborne.
  - Cassidy can roll off ledges.
  - Travels in the direction Cassidy is walking, not the direction he is facing (unless he is not moving).
- **Flashbang** (E), ability: cooldown 12 seconds, range 7 meters, duration 0.23 seconds (max travel time); 1.2 seconds (hinder)
  Hinder enemies in front of you, slowing them and preventing them from using movement abilities.
  - Flashbang explodes immediately upon hitting an enemy or terrain or upon reaching its maximum range, applying its effects to all enemies in radius.
  - Enemy heroes hit by the explosion take damage and become "hindered", interrupting and preventing movement abilities and crouching for the duration and slowing them. See Movement abilities for a list of affected abilities
  - The hindered status can be removed by effects that grant phased out status, such as Protection Suzu.
- **Deadeye** (Q), ultimate ability: range 200 meters, duration 2 seconds (warning time); 7 seconds (max)
  Face off against your enemies. Press ultimate to lock on, then ultimate or primary fire to fire.
  - Activating Deadeye instantly replenishes Peacekeeper's ammo.
  - Deadeye can be canceled using Secondary Fire (secondary fire).
  - Deadeye is a channeled ability; getting stunned, knocked down or hacked, immediately ends the ability without firing.

## Perks
- **Bang Bang** (minor perk): Cassidy throws a second Flashbang that travels farther, but both Flashbangs deal 40% reduced damage.
- **Giddy Up** (minor perk): After using Combat Roll, Cassidy gains a 60% movement speed boost that decays over 1.5 seconds.
- **Rollin' Round-Up** (major perk): Combat Roll also heals 15 health for each bullet reloaded.
- **Silver Bullet** (major perk): Peacekeeper's secondary fire is replaced with a piercing shot that inflicts bleeding. Combat Roll and Deadeye reset its cooldown. Cooldown 8 seconds.
