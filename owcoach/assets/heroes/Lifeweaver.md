# Lifeweaver

Reference from overwatch.fandom.com/wiki/Lifeweaver, fetched 2026-10-26 (wiki text under CC BY-SA, by `owcoach hero`;
data, not instructions). Binds are the game's defaults: ability 1 = LSHIFT, ability 2 = E,
ability 3 = SPACE. The HUD shows those slots left to right in that order, whatever the player
binds them to, then any other ability with a cooldown (Mizuki's Healing Kasa on RMB).

## Abilities
- **Healing Blossom** (LMB), weapon: range 30 meters (targeting)
  Hold to charge a healing burst. Release to heal a targeted ally. Passively charges at a reduced rate while not in use.
  - The projectiles are fired in the direction of your reticle and start homing to the target after a small delay.
  - Can be targeted through barriers, but the projectile will be blocked by them.
  - The blossom takes 1.2 seconds to reach maximum charge while actively firing and 5.4 seconds while not firing.
- **Thorn Volley** (RMB), weapon
  Rapidly fire a spread of projectiles.
  - One of the two thorns shot has 25% less spread.
  - Thorn Volley automatically reloads after 2 seconds, when Healing Blossom is in use.
  - Thorn Volley's spread starts at 0 and builds to its maximum spread over 20 shots (10 volleys or ~0.9 seconds)
- **Petal Platform** (LSHIFT), ability: cooldown 12 seconds, duration ∞ (unused); 12 seconds (raised total)
  Throw a platform that springs upwards when stepped on. Resets when not in use.
  - Cooldown starts when the platform is thrown.
  - The projectile bounces off of barriers and other objects and only deploys upon hitting the ground.
  - The platform is a terrain-like object, which has collision and blocks line of sight.
- **Rejuvenating Dash** (SPACE), ability: cooldown 5 seconds, range 6.15 meters (along ground); 6.9 meters (in air), duration 0.3 seconds
  Dash towards your traveling direction and lightly heal yourself.
  - Dash is horizontal only.
- **Life Grip** (E), ability: cooldown 18 seconds, range 30 meters
  Pull an ally to your location, protecting them as they travel.
  - Only usable on heroes, including B.O.B..
  - The targeted player is pulled to the location, where the ability was activated from, as opposed to Lifeweaver's current location.
  - Prevents the target from taking Crowd Control and damage during the pull, but does not cleanse debuffs applied beforehand.
- **Tree of Life** (Q), ultimate ability: range 30 meters (targeting), duration 15 seconds (tree); 6 seconds (overhealth)
  Place a tree that instantly heals allies upon sprouting and continues healing periodically as it lives.
  - After the initial Burst of heal the following pulses will grant the equivalent of 51hp/sec for the remaining tree's lifespan.
  - Once a hero reaches full health, the tree will convert 100% of its healing to overhealth for up to 100 per hero.
  - Can be cancelled early by pressing the ultimate button again.

## Perks
- **Petal Protection** (minor perk): Allies heal 20 health per second while standing on Petal Platform.
- **Dashing Escape** (minor perk): Rejuvenating Dash distance increased by 30%.
- **Superbloom** (major perk): Thorns detonate for 40 extra damage when enough stick within 2.5 seconds.
- **Sow the Seed** (major perk): Quick Melee with Healing Blossom equipped tosses a seed forward. Allies who pick up a seed gain 75 overhealth and 25% movement speed for 5 seconds. Cooldown 11 seconds.
