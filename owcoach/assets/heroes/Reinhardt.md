## Abilities
- **Rocket Hammer** (LMB), weapon: range 4 meters, duration 0.208 seconds (hitbox active)
  Devastating melee weapon.
  - Damage per second: ~104 while swinging
  - Reinhard does not have a quick melee attack. Attempting to use it swings the Rocket Hammer instead.
  - Rocket Hammer knocks back enemies in the direction of the swing.
- **Barrier Field** (RMB), ability: cooldown 5 seconds (destroyed)
  Hold secondary fire to deploy a frontal energy barrier.
  - Using Fire Strike, Charge or Earthshatter automatically puts down the barrier to cast the ability. To use Rocket Hammer, the barrier must be first manually released.
  - Barrier health regenerates at a rate of 150 per second after being down for 2 seconds.
  - Barrier Field is a channeled ability, If Reinhardt is hacked, slept, stunned and knocked down, it will be released.
- **Charge** (LSHIFT), ability: cooldown 7 seconds, range 50.2 meters, duration 3 seconds (charge, max); 0.3 seconds (charge, min); 0.65 seconds (stun on wall hit)
  Charge forward and smash an enemy against a wall.
  - Reinhardt can cancel the charge by pressing the ability button again.
  - Reinhardt can turn while charging but his control is reduced.

- **Fire Strike** (E), ability: cooldown 6 seconds, 2 charges
  Launch a fiery projectile.
  - Fire Strike stops if it collides with terrain or a terrain-like object, like Ice Wall.
- **Earthshatter** (Q), ultimate ability: duration 3 seconds (knockdown)
  Knock down all enemies in front of you.
  - Requires line of sight.
  - Earthshatter has a small amount of vertical height (1.77 meters), allowing it to affect enemies in the air.
  - Earthshatter is a channeled ability and it is interrupted by becoming hacked, stunned or knocked down.

## Perks
- **Crusader's Fire** (minor perk): Refund a charge of Fire Strike When you stun an enemy, overfilling up to 3.
- **Crusader's Resolve** (minor perk): While using Barrier Field, your passive health regeneration triggers 75% sooner.
- **Shield Slam** (major perk): While Barrier Field is active, use Primary Fire to damage and knockback enemies. Cooldown 6 seconds.
- **Ignited Fury** (major perk): For each enemy you hit with Fire Strike, gain 2 seconds of 25% increased attack speed.
