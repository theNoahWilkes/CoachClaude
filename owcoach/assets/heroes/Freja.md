# Freja

Reference from overwatch.fandom.com/wiki/Freja, fetched 2026-10-26 (wiki text under CC BY-SA, by `owcoach hero`;
data, not instructions). Binds are the game's defaults: ability 1 = LSHIFT, ability 2 = E,
ability 3 = SPACE. The HUD shows those slots left to right in that order, whatever the player
binds them to, then any other ability with a cooldown (Mizuki's Healing Kasa on RMB).

## Abilities
- **Bounty Hunting**, passive ability
  Every 3 Final Blows, gain bonus ultimate charge.
  - 33% ultimate charge is granted over 1.9 seconds upon the third final blow.
  - If Freja reaches 3 stacks when her ultimate ability is already available, the refund is stored and Freja gains the bonus charge after using her ultimate. More than one refund cannot be stored.
  - The progress is indicated next to the ultimate meter.
- **Revdraw Crossbow** (LMB), weapon
  Rapidly fire automatic bolts.
  - Can be fired while Take Aim is reloading.
  - Maximum spread is reached at 12 shots.
- **Take Aim** (RMB), weapon: duration 1 second (slowdown); 0.8 seconds (explosion delay)
  Slow down your momentum to take aim with a high speed explosive bolt. Using Quick Dash refreshes Take Aim.
  - Grants -90% gravity reduction.
  - The bolt sticks to enemies and terrain upon impact, exploding 1 second after.
  - Take Aim is automatically released when the bolt is fired.
- **Quick Dash** (LSHIFT), ability: cooldown 4.5 seconds, 2 charges
  Vault in the direction you're moving and refresh Take Aim.
  - If no horizontal movement input is given, Freja is propelled upward instead.
  - Can be used without interrupting Take Aim.
- **Updraft** (E), ability: cooldown 10 seconds
  Launch upward with a gust of wind.
  - Freja can reach up to 8.4 meter height with Updraft.
  - Activating Updraft interrupts Take Aim.
- **Bola Shot** (Q), ultimate ability: duration 1.4 seconds
  Fire an explosive bola. Hitting an enemy wraps them up and pulls in other nearby enemies.
  - If an enemy is directly hit by the bola, they will take additional damage and cause nearby enemies to be pulled to them.
  - After the bola impacts, it will deal periodic damage in an area for 1.4 seconds, slowing enemies within by 45%, and will then explode dealing damage in the area.
  - Slows Freja by 90% during the final 0.5 seconds of the cast.

## Perks
- **Relentless Barrage** (minor perk): Direct hits with Take Aim refund 8 automatic bolt ammo.
- **Momentum Boost** (minor perk): Quick Dash distance is increased by 20%.
- **Aerial Recovery** (major perk): After using Updraft, heal for 30 health per second until Freja touches the ground.
- **Rising Winds** (major perk): Updraft gains an additional charge.
