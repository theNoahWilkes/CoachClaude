# Widowmaker

Reference from overwatch.fandom.com/wiki/Widowmaker, fetched 2026-10-26 (wiki text under CC BY-SA, by `owcoach hero`;
data, not instructions). Binds are the game's defaults: ability 1 = LSHIFT, ability 2 = E,
ability 3 = SPACE. The HUD shows those slots left to right in that order, whatever the player
binds them to, then any other ability with a cooldown (Mizuki's Healing Kasa on RMB).

## Abilities
- **Widow's Kiss** (LMB), weapon
  Automatic assault weapon.
  - Damage per second: 140 while firing (98 overall w/ reload)
  - Maximum spread is reached after 11 shots.
- **Widow's Kiss (ADS)** (RMB), weapon
  Hold for long-ranged sniper weapon.
  - Damage per second: 80 while firing (full charge) (70 overall w/reload)
  - The base damage is 12 and scales linearly to 120. Each percent of power increases the damage by 1.08.
  - The minimum power required to kill a full health target less than 50 meters away (without armor) with a single headshot:
- **Grappling Hook** (LSHIFT), ability: cooldown 10 seconds, range 20 meters
  Launch a hook that pulls you towards a ledge.
  - Grappling Hook can be cancelled by pressing the jump key.
  - Grappling Hook can attach to Lifeweaver's Petal Platform and Tree of Life.
  - Will attempt to go around fences and other breakables when grappling through them, but can break them if they are in the way.
- **Venom Mine** (E), ability: cooldown 15 seconds, duration Until replaced or destroyed (mine); 1.4 seconds (arming time); 5 seconds (poison)
  Launch a poison trap.
  - Enemies who trigger the mine can be seen by Widowmaker through walls for a short time.
  - The venom effect is removed immediately upon taking a Health Pack.
  - If the ability is interrupted before cast time ends, the ability's cooldown will not trigger.
- **Infra-Sight** (Q), ultimate ability: duration 15 seconds
  Provide your team with a view of the enemy's location.
  - Allows Widowmaker's entire team to see enemies through walls and their health bars.
  - Infra-Sight only ends if Widowmaker is eliminated or once the duration runs out.

## Perks
- **Scoped Efficiency** (minor perk): Scoped shots cost 3 ammo instead of 5.
- **Sniper's Instinct** (minor perk): Scoped shots charge 100% faster for 2 seconds after using Grappling Hook.
- **Widow’s Bite** (major perk): Scoped shots can charge up to 125%, piercing enemies when fully charged.
- **Seeker Mine** (major perk): Venom Mine now fires poison darts at enemies within 10 meters and remains active after triggered.
