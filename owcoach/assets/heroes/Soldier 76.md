# Soldier: 76

Reference from overwatch.fandom.com/wiki/Soldier:_76, fetched 2026-10-26 (wiki text under CC BY-SA, by `owcoach hero`;
data, not instructions). Binds are the game's defaults: ability 1 = LSHIFT, ability 2 = E,
ability 3 = SPACE. The HUD shows those slots left to right in that order, whatever the player
binds them to, then any other ability with a cooldown (Mizuki's Healing Kasa on RMB).

## Abilities
- **Heavy Pulse Rifle** (LMB), weapon
  Automatic assault weapon.
  - Damage per second: 171 while firing (117.9 overall w/ reload)
  - Each shot changes facing angle +0.098.
- **Helix Rockets** (RMB), ability: cooldown 6 seconds
  Launch a volley of explosive rockets.
  - The explosion also applies a knockback.
  - Soldier: 76 takes 50% of the splash damage and will be affected by the knockback, if caught in the explosion.
  - The projectile can be destroyed while in air by Defense Matrix, Kinetic Grasp, Javelin Spin, Power Matrix and Kekkai Sanctuary and reflected by Deflect, but the explosion is unaffected.
- **Sprint** (LSHIFT), ability: duration ∞
  Run faster while moving forward.
  - After Sprint is cancelled, there is a 0.3 second recovery period where Soldier: 76 cannot use primary or secondary fire.
  - The activation of the ability can be customized between holding the button and toggling with a single press.
  - Sprint's movement speed buff does not count towards global movement speed buff cap.
- **Biotic Field** (E), ability: cooldown 15 seconds, duration 5 seconds
  Deploy a field that heals you and your allies.
  - Cannot be destroyed.
  - Only one field can be active at a time.
  - Healing is only applied to targets within line of sight of the biotic emitter, ignoring enemy barriers.
- **Tactical Visor** (Q), ultimate ability: range 45 meters, duration 6 seconds
  Automatically aims your weapon at targets in view.
  - Activation of Tactical Visor instantly replenishes Heavy Pulse Rifle's ammo and cancels the recovery animation of Quick Melee, Helix Rockets, Biotic Field and Stim Pack.
  - Heavy Pulse Rifle's reload time is greatly reduced while Tactical Visor is active.
  - Damage falloff still applies.

## Perks
- **Helix Propulsion** (minor perk): Helix Rockets' projectile speed is increased by 50%.
- **Tactical Salvo** (minor perk): During Tactical Visor, Helix Rockets' cooldown is reduced by 80% and no longer interrupts firing Heavy Pulse Rifle. Cooldown 6 → 1.2 seconds.
- **Full Stride** (major perk): Sprint's movement speed bonus gradually increases by an additional 25% over 2 seconds.
- **Stim Pack** (major perk): Stim Pack replaces Biotic Field. On use, Soldier: 76 heals for 30 health per second and gains 20% increased attack speed for 5 seconds. Cooldown 10 seconds.
