# Roadhog

Reference from overwatch.fandom.com/wiki/Roadhog, fetched 2026-10-26 (wiki text under CC BY-SA, by `owcoach hero`;
data, not instructions). Binds are the game's defaults: ability 1 = LSHIFT, ability 2 = E,
ability 3 = SPACE. The HUD shows those slots left to right in that order, whatever the player
binds them to, then any other ability with a cooldown (Mizuki's Healing Kasa on RMB).

## Abilities
- **Scrap Gun** (LMB), weapon
  Short-range spread weapon.
  - Damage per second: while firing ( overall w/ reload)
  - Fire recovery stops while using melee.
  - Primary Fire fires 2 bursts per volley
- **Trash Compactor** (RMB), weapon: cooldown 10s
  Absorb enemy projectiles, compacting them into an explosive ball of scrap.
- **Chain Hook** (LSHIFT), ability: cooldown 6 seconds, range 20 meters, duration 0.3 seconds (initial); 0.4 seconds (final)
  Drag a targeted enemy to you.
  - Cooldown starts when Chain Hook ends.
  - Hooked targets are released if they leave Roadhog’s line of sight before being pulled.
  - Chain Hook is a channeled ability; Roadhog cannot shoot or use other abilities while it is active, and it ends immediately if Roadhog becomes stunned, knocked down or hacked, including their subsidiary effects.
- **Take a Breather** (E), ability: cooldown 1.25 seconds, duration 0.4 seconds (min); 2.5 seconds (max); 2 seconds (energy regen delay)
  Heal yourself and reduce damage taken.
  - The activation is toggled, pressing the ability key again, firing or using Chain Hook automatically stops channeling the ability.
  - Recharge time is unaffected by Kitsune Rush, cooldown reduction or being affected by crowd control.
  - Take a Breather is a channeled ability; Roadhog cannot fire or use other abilities while it is active, and it ends immediately if Roadhog becomes stunned, knocked down or hacked, including their subsidiary effects.
- **Whole Hog** (Q), ultimate ability: duration 7.5 seconds
  Damage and knock back enemies in front of you.
  - Damage per second: 48 per pellet, 768 total.
  - Whole Hog transforms Roadhog weapon:
  - While firing Whole Hog, Roadhog receives a 25% movement speed penalty and cannot crouch.

## Perks
- **Scrap Hook** (minor perk): Chain Hook hits reload 4 ammo.
- **Shrapnel Launcher** (minor perk): Extend the range of Scrap Gun's secondary fire by 50% and tighten its burst spread by 25%.
- **Hogdrogen Exposure** (major perk): Take A Breather also heals nearby allies for 50% of its healing.
- **Pulled Pork** (major perk): Gain overhealth based on how far you pull enemies with Chain Hook, up to 300.
