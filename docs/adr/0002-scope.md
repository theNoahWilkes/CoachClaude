# Scope: look back at a session, locally, through the page

CoachClaude looks back at a session of matches and says what the player did well, what they
did poorly and what to try next, and it keeps a record of that play (the match log, plus the
lessons file). It is a personal project built to a shippable standard, so that it could be
shared one day.

- **More games** are in scope, but not a priority now.
- **A cloud service** is out of scope: hosting and model costs are unreasonable for a passion
  project. Everything runs on the player's machine.
- **Live, in-game coaching** is out of scope: anything that watches a running game risks being
  treated as a cheat by anti-cheat systems. The coach only reads finished recordings and
  replays.
- **The page is the only GUI.** Any visual polish goes into the page, not into a desktop app or
  overlay.
